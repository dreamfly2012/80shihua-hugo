<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">This guide extends and expands on&nbsp;<a href="https://github.com/php-fig/fig-standards/blob/master/accepted/PSR-1-basic-coding-standard.md" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(236, 134, 24); text-decoration-line: none;">PSR-1</a>, the basic coding standard.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The intent of this guide is to reduce cognitive friction when scanning code from different authors. It does so by enumerating a shared set of rules and expectations about how to format PHP code.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The style rules herein are derived from commonalities among the various member projects. When various authors collaborate across multiple projects, it helps to have one set of guidelines to be used among all those projects. Thus, the benefit of this guide is not in the rules themselves, but in the sharing of those rules.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The key words “MUST”, “MUST NOT”, “REQUIRED”, “SHALL”, “SHALL NOT”, “SHOULD”, “SHOULD NOT”, “RECOMMENDED”, “MAY”, and “OPTIONAL” in this document are to be interpreted as described in&nbsp;<a href="http://www.ietf.org/rfc/rfc2119.txt" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(236, 134, 24); text-decoration-line: none;">RFC 2119</a>.</p>
<h2 id="1-overview" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 40px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-weight: 400; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 24px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Oswald, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#1-overview" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); text-decoration-line: none;">1. Overview</a></h2>
<ul style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 20px; padding: 0px 0px 0px 30px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; list-style-position: initial; list-style-image: initial; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);" class=" list-paddingleft-2">
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">Code MUST follow a “coding style guide” PSR [<a href="https://github.com/php-fig/fig-standards/blob/master/accepted/PSR-1-basic-coding-standard.md" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(236, 134, 24); text-decoration-line: none;">PSR-1</a>].</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">Code MUST use 4 spaces for indenting, not tabs.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">There MUST NOT be a hard limit on line length; the soft limit MUST be 120 characters; lines SHOULD be 80 characters or less.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">There MUST be one blank line after the&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">namespace</code>&nbsp;declaration, and there MUST be one blank line after the block of&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">use</code>declarations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">Opening braces for classes MUST go on the next line, and closing braces MUST go on the next line after the body.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">Opening braces for methods MUST go on the next line, and closing braces MUST go on the next line after the body.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">Visibility MUST be declared on all properties and methods;&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">abstract</code>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">final</code>&nbsp;MUST be declared before the visibility;&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">static</code>MUST be declared after the visibility.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">Control structure keywords MUST have one space after them; method and function calls MUST NOT.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">Opening braces for control structures MUST go on the same line, and closing braces MUST go on the next line after the body.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">Opening parentheses for control structures MUST NOT have a space after them, and closing parentheses for control structures MUST NOT have a space before.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="11-example" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 35px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#11-example" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); text-decoration-line: none;">1.1. Example</a></h3>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">This example encompasses some of the rules below as a quick overview:</p>

General</a></p>
<h3 id="21-basic-coding-standard" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 35px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#21-basic-coding-standard" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); text-decoration-line: none;">2.1. Basic Coding Standard</a></h3>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Code MUST follow all rules outlined in&nbsp;<a href="https://github.com/php-fig/fig-standards/blob/master/accepted/PSR-1-basic-coding-standard.md" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(236, 134, 24); text-decoration-line: none;">PSR-1</a>.</p>
<h3 id="22-files" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 35px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#22-files" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); text-decoration-line: none;">2.2. Files</a></h3>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">All PHP files MUST use the Unix LF (linefeed) line ending.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">All PHP files MUST end with a single blank line.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The closing&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">?&gt;</code>&nbsp;tag MUST be omitted from files containing only PHP.</p>
<h3 id="23-lines" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 35px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#23-lines" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); text-decoration-line: none;">2.3. Lines</a></h3>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">There MUST NOT be a hard limit on line length.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The soft limit on line length MUST be 120 characters; automated style checkers MUST warn but MUST NOT error at the soft limit.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Lines SHOULD NOT be longer than 80 characters; lines longer than that SHOULD be split into multiple subsequent lines of no more than 80 characters each.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">There MUST NOT be trailing whitespace at the end of non-blank lines.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Blank lines MAY be added to improve readability and to indicate related blocks of code.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">There MUST NOT be more than one statement per line.</p>
<h3 id="24-indenting" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 35px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#24-indenting" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); text-decoration-line: none;">2.4. Indenting</a></h3>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Code MUST use an indent of 4 spaces, and MUST NOT use tabs for indenting.</p>
<blockquote style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; quotes: none; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline;">N.b.: Using only spaces, and not mixing spaces with tabs, helps to avoid problems with diffs, patches, history, and annotations. The use of spaces also makes it easy to insert fine-grained sub-indentation for inter-line alignment.</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="25-keywords-and-truefalsenull" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 35px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#25-keywords-and-truefalsenull" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); text-decoration-line: none;">2.5. Keywords and True/False/Null</a></h3>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">PHP&nbsp;<a href="http://php.net/manual/en/reserved.keywords.php" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(236, 134, 24); text-decoration-line: none;">keywords</a>&nbsp;MUST be in lower case.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The PHP constants&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">true</code>,&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">false</code>, and&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">null</code>&nbsp;MUST be in lower case.</p>
<h2 id="3-namespace-and-use-declarations" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 40px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-weight: 400; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 24px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Oswald, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#3-namespace-and-use-declarations" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); text-decoration-line: none;">3. Namespace and Use Declarations</a></h2>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">When present, there MUST be one blank line after the&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">namespace</code>&nbsp;declaration.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">When present, all&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">use</code>&nbsp;declarations MUST go after the&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">namespace</code>&nbsp;declaration.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">There MUST be one&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">use</code>&nbsp;keyword per declaration.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">There MUST be one blank line after the&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">use</code>&nbsp;block.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">For example:</p>
<pre style="background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255); font-family: Consolas; font-size: 13.5pt;"><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">&lt;?php<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">namespace </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">Vendor\Package;<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"><br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">FooClass;<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">BarClass </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">as </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">Bar;<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">OtherVendor\OtherPackage\BazClass;<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"><br /></span><span style="color:#808080;background-color:#f7faff;font-style:italic;">// ... additional PHP code ...</span></pre>
<h2 id="4-classes-properties-and-methods" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 40px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-weight: 400; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 24px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Oswald, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#4-classes-properties-and-methods" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); text-decoration-line: none;">4. Classes, Properties, and Methods</a></h2>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The term “class” refers to all classes, interfaces, and traits.</p>
<h3 id="41-extends-and-implements" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 35px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#41-extends-and-implements" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); text-decoration-line: none;">4.1. Extends and Implements</a></h3>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">extends</code>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">implements</code>&nbsp;keywords MUST be declared on the same line as the class name.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The opening brace for the class MUST go on its own line; the closing brace for the class MUST go on the next line after the body.</p>
<p></p>
<pre style="background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255); font-family: Consolas; font-size: 13.5pt;"><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">&lt;?php<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">namespace </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">Vendor\Package;<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"><br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">FooClass;<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">BarClass </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">as </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">Bar;<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">OtherVendor\OtherPackage\BazClass;<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"><br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">class </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">ClassName </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">extends </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">ParentClass </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">implements </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">\ArrayAccess, \Countable<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">{<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;</span><span style="color:#808080;background-color:#f7faff;font-style:italic;">// constants, properties, methods<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">}</span></pre>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Lists of&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">implements</code>&nbsp;MAY be split across multiple lines, where each subsequent line is indented once. When doing so, the first item in the list MUST be on the next line, and there MUST be only one interface per line.</p>
<p></p>
<pre style="background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255); font-family: Consolas; font-size: 13.5pt;"><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">&lt;?php<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">namespace </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">Vendor\Package;<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"><br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">FooClass;<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">BarClass </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">as </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">Bar;<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">use </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">OtherVendor\OtherPackage\BazClass;<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"><br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">class </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">ClassName </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">extends </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">ParentClass </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">implements<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;</span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">\ArrayAccess,<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;\Countable,<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;\Serializable<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">{<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;</span><span style="color:#808080;background-color:#f7faff;font-style:italic;">// constants, properties, methods<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">}</span></pre>
<h3 id="42-properties" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 35px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#42-properties" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); text-decoration-line: none;">4.2. Properties</a></h3>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Visibility MUST be declared on all properties.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">var</code>&nbsp;keyword MUST NOT be used to declare a property.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">There MUST NOT be more than one property declared per statement.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Property names SHOULD NOT be prefixed with a single underscore to indicate protected or private visibility.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">A property declaration looks like the following.</p>
<p></p>

<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Visibility MUST be declared on all methods.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Method names SHOULD NOT be prefixed with a single underscore to indicate protected or private visibility.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Method names MUST NOT be declared with a space after the method name. There MUST NOT be a space after the opening parenthesis, and there MUST NOT be a space before the closing parenthesis.</p>

<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">When present, the&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">abstract</code>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">final</code>&nbsp;declarations MUST precede the visibility declaration.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">When present, the&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">static</code>&nbsp;declaration MUST come after the visibility declaration.</p>

<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">When making a method or function call, there MUST NOT be a space between the method or function name and the opening parenthesis, there MUST NOT be a space after the opening parenthesis, and there MUST NOT be a space before the closing parenthesis. <h2 id="5-control-structures" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 40px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-weight: 400; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 24px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Oswald, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#5-control-structures" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); text-decoration-line: none;">5. Control Structures</a></h2>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The general style rules for control structures are as follows:</p>
<ul style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 20px; padding: 0px 0px 0px 30px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; list-style-position: initial; list-style-image: initial; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);" class=" list-paddingleft-2">
<li>
<p>There MUST be one space after the control structure keyword</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>There MUST NOT be a space after the opening parenthesis</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>There MUST NOT be a space before the closing parenthesis</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>There MUST be one space between the closing parenthesis and the opening brace</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The structure body MUST be indented once</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The closing brace MUST be on the next line after the body</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The body of each structure MUST be enclosed by braces. <p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">A&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">try catch</code>&nbsp;block looks like the following. Note the placement of parentheses, spaces, and braces.</p>
<p></p>
<pre style="background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255); font-family: Consolas; font-size: 13.5pt;"><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">&lt;?php<br /></span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">try </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">{<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;</span><span style="color:#808080;background-color:#f7faff;font-style:italic;">// try body<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">} </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">catch </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">(FirstExceptionType </span><span style="color:#660000;background-color:#f7faff;">$e</span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">) {<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;</span><span style="color:#808080;background-color:#f7faff;font-style:italic;">// catch body<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">} </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">catch </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">(OtherExceptionType </span><span style="color:#660000;background-color:#f7faff;">$e</span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">) {<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;</span><span style="color:#808080;background-color:#f7faff;font-style:italic;">// catch body<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">}</span></pre>
<h2 id="6-closures" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 40px 0px 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-weight: 400; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 24px; line-height: 1.25; font-family: Oswald, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);"><a href="https://www.php-fig.org/psr/psr-2/#6-closures" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font: inherit; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(62, 163, 119); text-decoration-line: none;">6. Closures</a></h2>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Closures MUST be declared with a space after the&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">function</code>&nbsp;keyword, and a space before and after the&nbsp;<code class="highlighter-rouge" style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin: 0px; padding: 3px 5px; border: 1px solid rgb(242, 235, 222); font-style: inherit; font-variant: inherit; font-weight: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 16px; line-height: inherit; font-family: &quot;Source Code Pro&quot;, monospace; vertical-align: baseline; letter-spacing: -0.02em; background: white;">use</code>&nbsp;keyword.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The opening brace MUST go on the same line, and the closing brace MUST go on the next line following the body.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">There MUST NOT be a space after the opening parenthesis of the argument list or variable list, and there MUST NOT be a space before the closing parenthesis of the argument list or variable list.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">In the argument list and variable list, there MUST NOT be a space before each comma, and there MUST be one space after each comma.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Closure arguments with default values MUST go at the end of the argument list.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">A closure declaration looks like the following. Note the placement of parentheses, commas, spaces, and braces:</p>
<p></p>
<pre style="background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255); font-family: Consolas; font-size: 13.5pt;"><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">&lt;?php<br /></span><span style="color:#660000;background-color:#f7faff;">$closureWithArgs </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">= </span><span style="color:#000080;background-color:#f7faff;font-weight:bold;">function </span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">(</span><span style="color:#660000;background-color:#f7faff;">$arg1</span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">, </span><span style="color:#660000;background-color:#f7faff;">$arg2</span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">) {<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;"> &nbsp; &nbsp;</span><span style="color:#808080;background-color:#f7faff;font-style:italic;">// body<br /></span><span style="background-color:#f7faff;">};</span></pre>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">Argument lists and variable lists MAY be split across multiple lines, where each subsequent line is indented once. When doing so, the first item in the list MUST be on the next line, and there MUST be only one argument or variable per line.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">When the ending list (whether of arguments or variables) is split across multiple lines, the closing parenthesis and opening brace MUST be placed together on their own line with one space between them.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 25px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; font-size: 18px; line-height: inherit; font-family: Roboto, sans-serif; vertical-align: baseline; color: rgb(89, 81, 67); white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(249, 246, 240);">The following are examples of closures with and without argument lists and variable lists split across multiple lines.</p>
