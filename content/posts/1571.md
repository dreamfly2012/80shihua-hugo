<p>apache的许多功能都是通过模块来做的，比如支持php,就是有个php模块来处理php程序，那么压缩也是一样，也是通过一个模块来处理，这个模块就是<span style="color: rgb(79, 79, 79); font-family: -apple-system, &quot;SF UI Text&quot;, Arial, &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Hiragino Sans GB&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, &quot;WenQuanYi Micro Hei&quot;, sans-serif; font-variant-ligatures: common-ligatures; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">mod_deflates.so</span></p>
<p><span style="color: rgb(79, 79, 79); font-family: -apple-system, &quot;SF UI Text&quot;, Arial, &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Hiragino Sans GB&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, &quot;WenQuanYi Micro Hei&quot;, sans-serif; font-variant-ligatures: common-ligatures; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><br /></span></p>
<p><span style="color: rgb(79, 79, 79); font-family: -apple-system, &quot;SF UI Text&quot;, Arial, &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Hiragino Sans GB&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, &quot;WenQuanYi Micro Hei&quot;, sans-serif; font-variant-ligatures: common-ligatures; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">只要在httpd.conf里面引入mod_deflates.so这个模块，然后添加配置就可以了<br /></span></p>
<p><span style="color: rgb(79, 79, 79); font-family: -apple-system, &quot;SF UI Text&quot;, Arial, &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Hiragino Sans GB&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, &quot;WenQuanYi Micro Hei&quot;, sans-serif; font-variant-ligatures: common-ligatures; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><br /></span></p>
<p><span style="color: rgb(79, 79, 79); font-family: -apple-system, &quot;SF UI Text&quot;, Arial, &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Hiragino Sans GB&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, &quot;WenQuanYi Micro Hei&quot;, sans-serif; font-variant-ligatures: common-ligatures; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"></span></p>
<p>&lt;IfModule mod_deflate.c&gt;</p>
<p>AddOutputFilterByType DEFLATE text/*</p>
<p>AddOutputFilterByType DEFLATE application/ms* application/vnd* application/postscript</p>
<p>application/javascript application/x-javascript</p>
<p>AddOutputFilterByType DEFLATE application/x-httpd-php application/x-httpd-fastphp</p>
<p>&lt;/IfModule&gt;</p>
