<p>You&#39;re reading part two of&nbsp;<span style="font-family: &#39;Avenir Next LT W01 Bold&#39;, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif;"><a href="http://zachholman.com/posts/how-github-works" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); text-decoration: none; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-color: rgb(254, 205, 157); transition: border-color 0.2s ease; -webkit-transition: border-color 0.2s ease;">How GitHub Works</a></span>, a three-post journey into how we work at GitHub. They&#39;re all separate posts on different topics, but if you&#39;re interested you can also read the other two:&nbsp;<a href="http://zachholman.com/posts/how-github-works-hours/" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); text-decoration: none; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-color: rgb(254, 205, 157); transition: border-color 0.2s ease; -webkit-transition: border-color 0.2s ease;">Hours are Bullshit</a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="http://zachholman.com/posts/how-github-works-creativity/" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); text-decoration: none; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-color: rgb(254, 205, 157); transition: border-color 0.2s ease; -webkit-transition: border-color 0.2s ease;">Creativity is Important</a>.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">This is — by far — my favorite aspect of working at GitHub. Everything is asynchronous.</p>
<h2 id="chat" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); font-weight: normal; font-family: &#39;Avenir Next LT W01 Bold&#39;, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; white-space: normal;">Chat</h2>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">GitHub didn&#39;t have an office for the first two years. Chat rooms (in our case,&nbsp;<a href="http://campfirenow.com/" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); text-decoration: none; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-color: rgb(254, 205, 157); transition: border-color 0.2s ease; -webkit-transition: border-color 0.2s ease;">Campfire</a>) is where things got done. Today we&#39;ve moved into our second office, and Campfire is&nbsp;<span style="font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Italic, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif;">still</span>&nbsp;where we get things done. There&#39;s a reason for that: chat is asynchronous.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">Asynchronous communication means I can take a step out for lunch and catch up on transcripts when I get back. Asynchronous communication means I can ask my coworker a question in-chat and not worry about bothering her since she&#39;ll get back to me when she&#39;s available. Asynchronous communication means I can go to rural Minnesota and feel like I&#39;m working from the office like normal.</p>
<h2 id="pull-requests" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); font-weight: normal; font-family: &#39;Avenir Next LT W01 Bold&#39;, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; white-space: normal;">Pull Requests</h2>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">The majority of our development workflow involves&nbsp;<a href="https://github.com/features/projects/codereview#codereview_bucket" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); text-decoration: none; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-color: rgb(254, 205, 157); transition: border-color 0.2s ease; -webkit-transition: border-color 0.2s ease;">Pull Requests</a>. I&#39;m going to be going into a lot more detail about this in future blog posts<a href="http://zachholman.com/talks" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); text-decoration: none; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-color: rgb(254, 205, 157); transition: border-color 0.2s ease; -webkit-transition: border-color 0.2s ease;">and talks</a>&nbsp;but in the meantime let me say this: living in a pull request world is sublime. Gone are days at companies with complicated branching strategies, with in-person code-on-a-screen code review.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">If I want to add a new feature or impact the codebase, I&#39;ll push a new branch, create a Pull Request for it, and my coworkers will review it 1) if they&#39;re impacted by those changes, 2) interested in the subject, or 3) when they have ample time to check out my changes. At that point, we can run a partial deploy of that branch on different subsets of machines and try things out in production, and if everything looks good, merge into master.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">With Pull Requests, I don&#39;t have to drag anyone into a meeting that&#39;s inconvenient for them and for me. There&#39;s a good reason for that, too:</p>
<h2 id="meetings-are-fucking-toxic" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); font-weight: normal; font-family: &#39;Avenir Next LT W01 Bold&#39;, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; white-space: normal;">Meetings are fucking toxic</h2>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">37signals&nbsp;<a href="http://gettingreal.37signals.com/ch07_Meetings_Are_Toxic.php" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); text-decoration: none; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-color: rgb(254, 205, 157); transition: border-color 0.2s ease; -webkit-transition: border-color 0.2s ease;">originated</a>&nbsp;&quot;meetings are toxic&quot; in Getting Real. I tend to loathe meetings&nbsp;<span style="font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Italic, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif;">even more</span>&nbsp;than 37signals. I despise them.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">Meetings are usually called when you need to hash something out. They tend to invite more people than necessary, and even if you&#39;re pretty interested in the meeting topic, you&#39;re still going to be frustrated because&nbsp;<span style="color: rgb(26, 137, 138); font-family: &#39;Avenir Next LT W01 Bold&#39;, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif;">meetings pull you from doing actual work in order to talk about doing work</span>. It&#39;s easier to push a branch up, check out the diff, and then iterate on that diff rather than assuming you&#39;re going to perfectly whiteboard system design ahead of time.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">Beyond that, meetings are utterly forgettable. Even if you take meeting notes, you can&#39;t capture them all. You make a judgement at that point in time as to what to write down for later. Then three weeks later you try to remember what wasn&#39;t written down when it becomes apparent that&nbsp;<span style="font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Italic, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif;">that</span>&nbsp;discussion was more important. You don&#39;t have this problem with chat transcripts. Forcing people to reduce their otherwise rambling thoughts into concrete sentences helps focus discussion, too.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">We&#39;ll have meetings at GitHub, but I can count the number of full &quot;meetings&quot; we&#39;ve had in the last year and a half on one hand.</p>
<h2 id="the-zone" style="color: rgb(253, 127, 5); font-weight: normal; font-family: &#39;Avenir Next LT W01 Bold&#39;, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; white-space: normal;">The Zone</h2>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">This all goes back to what I was writing about yesterday: you want your employees to be in &quot;the zone&quot;. Putting people in an atmosphere where they can only work for an hour before a team meeting pulls them out of that flow.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; font-family: AvenirNextLTW01-Regular, &#39;Avenir Next&#39;, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; line-height: 32px; white-space: normal;">We&#39;ve found that if you let responsible people handle their own priorities on their own timeline, they&#39;ll end up getting the important stuff finished and still be able to work productively on other work.</p>
