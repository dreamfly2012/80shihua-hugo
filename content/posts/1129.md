<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">默认的</p>
<pre class="brush:xml;toolbar:false">&nbsp;

RewriteRule&nbsp;^(.*)$&nbsp;index.php/$1&nbsp;[QSA,PT,L]</pre>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="line-height: 21px;"></span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">规则在apache fastcgi模式下会导致No input file specified.</p>
<p><br style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">修改成</p>
<pre class="brush:xml;toolbar:false">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;RewriteRule&nbsp;^(.*)$&nbsp;index.php&nbsp;[L,E=PATH_INFO:$1]</pre>
<p><span style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"></span><br style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">就OK，地址正常重写。</p>
<p><br style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">我们都知道，使用伪静态相对来说，对搜索引擎比较友好，启用REWRITE的伪静态功能的时候，首页可以访问，而访问内页的时候，就提示：“No input file specified.”。</p>
<p><br style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">WordPress及Typecho等程序默认的.htaccess里面的规则为例：</p>
<pre class="brush:xml;toolbar:false">RewriteEngine&nbsp;On

RewriteBase&nbsp;/

RewriteCond&nbsp;%{REQUEST_FILENAME}&nbsp;!-f

RewriteCond&nbsp;%{REQUEST_FILENAME}&nbsp;!-d

RewriteRule&nbsp;^(.*)$&nbsp;/index.php/$1&nbsp;[L]</pre>
<p><span style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"></span><br style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">修改一下伪静态规则，如下：</p>
<pre class="brush:xml;toolbar:false">RewriteEngine&nbsp;On

RewriteBase&nbsp;/

RewriteCond&nbsp;%{REQUEST_FILENAME}&nbsp;!-f

RewriteCond&nbsp;%{REQUEST_FILENAME}&nbsp;!-d

RewriteRule&nbsp;^(.*)$&nbsp;/index.php?/$1&nbsp;[L]</pre>
<p><span style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"></span><br style="font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 15px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(37, 37, 37); line-height: 28px; text-indent: 2em; font-family: tahoma, arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">在正则结果“$1”前面多加了一个“?”号，问题也就随之解决了。</p>
