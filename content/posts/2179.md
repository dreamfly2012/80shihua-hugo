<p>现在很多网站，特别是小程序都要求https的访问，如果你不想花几百元买一个https的证书的话，那么下面的方法你一定要记得收藏。</p>
<p></p>
<ol class=" list-paddingleft-2" style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>
<p>安装acme.sh&nbsp;</p>
<p>curl <a href="https://get.acme.sh" _src="https://get.acme.sh">https://get.acme.sh</a>&nbsp;| sh</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>插入证书</p>
<p>acme.sh &#8211;issue -d example.com -w /home/wwwroot/example.com</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>nginx和apache安装证</p>
<p><strong>apache安装证书</strong></p>
<p>acme.sh &#8211;install-cert -d example.com \</p>
<p>&#8211;cert-file&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; /path/to/certfile/in/apache/cert.pem&nbsp; \</p>
<p>&#8211;key-file&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;/path/to/keyfile/in/apache/key.pem&nbsp; \</p>
<p>&#8211;fullchain-file /path/to/fullchain/certfile/apache/fullchain.pem \</p>
<p>&#8211;reloadcmd&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;&quot;service apache2 force-reload&quot;</p>
<p><strong>nginx安装证书</strong></p>
<p>acme.sh &#8211;install-cert -d example.com \</p>
<p>&#8211;key-file&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;/path/to/keyfile/in/nginx/key.pem&nbsp; \</p>
<p>&#8211;fullchain-file /path/to/fullchain/nginx/cert.pem \</p>
<p>&#8211;reloadcmd&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;&quot;service nginx force-reload&quot;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>60天过期之后重新生成</p>
<p>acme.sh &#8211;renew -d example.com &#8211;force</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p></p>
<p>就是这么简单，就是这么方便，其它的问题或者参数可以去<a href="https://github.com/Neilpang/acme.sh">https://github.com/Neilpang/acme.sh</a>查看</p>
