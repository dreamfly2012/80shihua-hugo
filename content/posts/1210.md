授权接口： $url          = "https://open.weixin.qq.com/connect/oauth2/authorize?appid=" . $appid . "&redirect_uri=" . $redirect_url . "&response_type=code&scope=snsapi_userinfo&state=123#wechat_redirect";

$appid 微信公众号appid

$redirect_url 授权后返回的页面，会在页面后面加上code参数

access_token信息获取接口

https://api.weixin.qq.com/sns/oauth2/access_token?appid=' . $appid . '&secret=' . $secret . '&code=' .$code . '&grant_type=authorization_code

$appid 微信公众号appid

$sercret 微信公众号秘钥

$code 授权返回来的数据

用户信息接口

$access_token 获取到的access_token

[https://api.weixin.qq.com/sns/userinfo?access_token=$access_token&openid=$openid&lang=zh_CN](https://api.weixin.qq.com/sns/userinfo?access_token=$access_token&openid=$openid&lang=zh_CN)
