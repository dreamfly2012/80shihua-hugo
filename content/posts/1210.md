<p>授权接口：&nbsp;$url&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; = &quot;https://open.weixin.qq.com/connect/oauth2/authorize?appid=&quot; . $appid . &quot;&amp;redirect_uri=&quot; . $redirect_url . &quot;&amp;response_type=code&amp;scope=snsapi_userinfo&amp;state=123#wechat_redirect&quot;;</p>
<p></p>
<p>$appid 微信公众号appid</p>
<p>$redirect_url 授权后返回的页面，会在页面后面加上code参数</p>
<p></p>
<p>access_token信息获取接口</p>
<p></p>
<p>https://api.weixin.qq.com/sns/oauth2/access_token?appid=&#39; . $appid . &#39;&amp;secret=&#39; . $secret . &#39;&amp;code=&#39; .$code . &#39;&amp;grant_type=authorization_code</p>
<p></p>
<p>$appid 微信公众号appid</p>
<p>$sercret 微信公众号秘钥</p>
<p>$code 授权返回来的数据</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>用户信息接口</p>
<p>$access_token 获取到的access_token</p>
<p></p>
<p><a href="https://api.weixin.qq.com/sns/userinfo?access_token=$access_token&#038;openid=$openid&#038;lang=zh_CN" _src="https://api.weixin.qq.com/sns/userinfo?access_token=$access_token&#038;openid=$openid&#038;lang=zh_CN">https://api.weixin.qq.com/sns/userinfo?access_token=$access_token&amp;openid=$openid&amp;lang=zh_CN</a> </p>
