<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">在软件开发的领域中，这个有害的编程习惯基本上都是由不良的编码习惯造成的，只有能改变这些不良习惯，你的工作起来会更轻松更有效率。</p>
<p class="article_img" style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; text-align: center; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-649" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/231849ef6s7sc7dtnsaqnz.jpg" width="658" height="439" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/231849ef6s7sc7dtnsaqnz.jpg 658w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/231849ef6s7sc7dtnsaqnz-300x200.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 658px) 100vw, 658px" /></p>
<p><a id="3_326" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">1.总出现错误的变量名和函数名拼写</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">让很多人想不通的是，为什么大家都知道这个习惯对自己没有好处，为什么还是有人让他出现，以至于常常出现错误的变量名和函数名的拼写。而且这些错误的拼写总是隐蔽的很好，也很难被发现。</p>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">想要解决这个问题，我们可以在一个比较成熟的开发环境上写代码，又或是用程序员专用的文本编辑器，这些都可以减少拼写错误。也可以用特定的变量名和函数名，这样容易拼写，也容易发现写错的地方。</p>
<p><a id="3_936" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">2.不按规定的格式写代码</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">常用的缩进和格式化能让写出的代码一目了然，有什么错误一看就知道。而且别人看着也方便。</p>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">倘若你使用的是不会自动格式化的代码，那你可以考虑用代码美化软件，如Uncrustify，因为他允许用户自定义格式要求，接着它会按你的要求执行任务。</p>
<p><a id="3_1322" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">3.不按规定的模块化编写代码</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">长函数实现的路径太多，常常测试起来就很麻烦，所以要习惯于一个函数对应一个指令，这样及简短又容易理解和维护。</p>
<p><a id="3_1539" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">4.不要总是依赖IDE</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">不用多说，大家都知道，IDE能让你的代码写的又好又快。他们能提你想用的很多东西和选择提示。但也会存在一定的风险，若你不能确保自己有足够的细心，那么很容易会出错。</p>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">好的工具的确是个不错的帮手，他可以消除拼写错误，还可以提高我们的工作效率，如果你还不够细心，那同样用了这些也还是会出问题。</p>
<p><a id="3_2008" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">5.总是写死密码</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">一些人总是喜欢在程序里写写死帐户名和密码，这样可以方便进入系统，虽然是方便了，但一样也方便了别人，所以这是不对的。</p>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">主要原因是硬编码远比你想的脆弱，如果不及时更正你的习惯，这将是个很大的而且不好修复的安全隐患。</p>
<p><a id="3_2369" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">6.没有使用加密保护数据</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">通常数据在互联网上传输都是需要加密的。不然在传输的过程中很可能被拦截。不要嫌麻烦，如果你不加密，出现了问题会让你更麻烦。如果写安全加密系统比较难，我们也可以使用经过验证过的标准加密库。</p>
<p><a id="3_2697" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">7.不要提前优化代码</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">善于写代码的程序员在写代码时的确是写的又快又顺畅，而后期的调试和维护则会难些。一定要想好策略，写好代码后再去找真正需要优化的地方以提高性能。</p>
<p><a id="3_2953" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">8.没有考虑好发展趋势</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">做这个项目的目的是什么？要做多大的规模？用户会是多少？也许有人认为这些好像和我们没什么关联，但是，如果你不好好考虑这些问题，怎么才能在刚开始时开发正确的程序框架。</p>
<p><a id="3_3245" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">9.不是人手越多进度越快</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">为什么每次要做的项目都比计划中的慢，当进度赶不上的时候，人们总是习惯性的增加人手，但事实上并不是增加了人手后进度就快了，有时反而是不升反降低了。</p>
<p><a id="3_3513" class="target-fix ext" rel="external nofollow" style="word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(77, 138, 216); position: relative; top: -40px; display: block; height: 0px; overflow: hidden; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; white-space: normal;"></a></p>
<h3 style="word-wrap: break-word; margin: 6px 0px 3px; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.1em; font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;, &#39;Hiragino Sans GB&#39;, STHeiti, Tahoma, SimHei, sans-serif; border-bottom-width: 1px; border-bottom-style: dotted; border-bottom-color: rgb(47, 47, 47); width: 714.594px; white-space: normal;">10.明知有问题还不变通</h3>
<p style="word-wrap: break-word; margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; line-height: 2em; font-family: Tahoma, Helvetica, SimSun, sans-serif; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal;">也有一些团队在做项目时发现进度远赶不上计划的时间时，就会做出拼命赶进度的情况。这样并不是办法，如果时间上真的比计划中要长很多，那么，说明你在对这个项目上预估的时间是有问题的。这时不要硬着头皮往上冲，应该从新调整时间才对。</p>
