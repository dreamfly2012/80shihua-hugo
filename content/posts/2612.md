<h2>安装依赖</h2>
<pre><code class="language-shell">composer require freyo/flysystem-qcloud-cos-v5</code></pre>
<h2>添加服务</h2>
<p>修改config/app.php文件,添加cos服务</p>
<pre><code class="language-php">Freyo\Flysystem\QcloudCOSv5\ServiceProvider::class,</code></pre>
<h2>添加文件存储驱动</h2>
<p>修改config/filesystems.php文件，添加cos配置</p>
<pre><code class="language-php">&#039;disks&#039;=&gt;[
    // ...
    &#039;cosv5&#039; =&gt; [
          &#039;driver&#039; =&gt; &#039;cosv5&#039;,
          &#039;region&#039;          =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_REGION&#039;, &#039;ap-guangzhou&#039;),
          &#039;credentials&#039;     =&gt; [
              &#039;appId&#039;     =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_APP_ID&#039;),
              &#039;secretId&#039;  =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_SECRET_ID&#039;),
              &#039;secretKey&#039; =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_SECRET_KEY&#039;),
          ],
          &#039;timeout&#039;         =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_TIMEOUT&#039;, 60),
          &#039;connect_timeout&#039; =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_CONNECT_TIMEOUT&#039;, 60),
          &#039;bucket&#039;          =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_BUCKET&#039;),
          &#039;cdn&#039;             =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_CDN&#039;),
          &#039;scheme&#039;          =&gt; env(&#039;COSV5_SCHEME&#039;, &#039;https&#039;),
    ],
],</code></pre>
<h2>添加配置</h2>
<p>修改.env文件</p>
<pre><code class="language-shell">COSV5_REGION=xxx
COSV5_APP_ID=xxx
COSV5_SECRET_ID=xxx
COSV5_SECRET_KEY=xxx
COSV5_TIMEOUT=xxx
COSV5_CONNECT_TIMEOUT=xxx
COSV5_BUCKET=xxx
COSV5_CDN=xxx
COSV5_SCHEME=xxx</code></pre>
<h2>编写调用存储代码</h2>
<pre><code class="language-php">$filename = $this-&gt;getFileName($request-&gt;image);获取文件名

$FilePath = $request-&gt;image-&gt;getRealPath(); //获取文件临时存放位置

Storage::disk(&#039;cosv5&#039;)-&gt;put($filename, file_get_contents($FilePath)); //云存储文件

$request-&gt;image-&gt;move(base_path(&#039;public/images&#039;), $filename);//本地保存文件</code></pre>
