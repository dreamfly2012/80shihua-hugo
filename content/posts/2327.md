<p>win10 系统中，打开power shell</p>
<p>输入下面的命令就可以快速安装好chocolatey</p>
<pre><code>Set-ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Scope Process -Force; iex ((New-Object System.Net.WebClient).DownloadString(&#039;https://chocolatey.org/install.ps1&#039;))</code></pre>
<p>安装之后，输入choco查看安装是否成功</p>
