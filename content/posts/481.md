<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">如果ping域名的时候出现ping:unknown host &nbsp;xxx.xxx</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-785" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1347371866_1962.jpg" width="545" height="50" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1347371866_1962.jpg 545w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1347371866_1962-300x28.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 545px) 100vw, 545px" /></span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">但是ping&nbsp;IP地址的时候可以通的话</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-786" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1347371995_6053.jpg" width="471" height="78" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1347371995_6053.jpg 471w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1347371995_6053-300x50.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 471px) 100vw, 471px" /></span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">可知是dns服务器没有配置好，</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">查看一下配置文件/etc/resolv.conf，里面是否有nameserver xxx.xxx.xxx.xxx，比如使用dns服务器 nameserver 8.8.8.8，如果有，修改一个可用的dns服务器，如8.8.8.8或者4.4.4.4，保存退出即可！</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><br /></span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><br /></span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">当然，如果连ip都ping 8.8.8.8都ping不通的话，那么就说明网络配置有问题：可以这样解决：</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">1，首先查看ifconfig是否有内网ip地址，如果没有，那么配置一下</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">ifconfig eth0 192.168.1.100 up，</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><img decoding="async" src="http://img.my.csdn.net/uploads/201209/21/1348198263_2817.jpg" alt="" style="border: none; max-width: 100%;"/></span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">可以同时配置好掩码：</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-787" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1348198443_8434.jpg" width="634" height="370" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1348198443_8434.jpg 634w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1348198443_8434-300x175.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 634px) 100vw, 634px" /><br /></span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">另外使用route命令查看一下当前的默认网关，如果没有，也配置一个，</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">route add default gw 192.168.1.1，</span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-788" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1348198304_4009.jpg" width="476" height="33" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1348198304_4009.jpg 476w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1348198304_4009-300x21.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 476px) 100vw, 476px" /></span></p>
<p style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Arial; font-size: 14px; line-height: 26px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-size: 18px;">配置好这两个以后，就可以正常的ping通网络了，如果还不能ping www.baidu.com,那么就要去查看dns服务器啦</span></p>
