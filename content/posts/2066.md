<p>这是面试中经常问到的已到经典问题，主要考察的是对php一些冷门函数的掌握程度。</p>
<p></p>
<p>解答这个问题之前，先来学习下2个不经常使用的php数组函数.</p>
<p></p>
<p>array_count_values 统计数组中值的个数</p>
<p>array_intersect_key 求两个数组的key的交集</p>
<p></p>
<p>通过这两个函数，我们就可以很轻松地计算出2个数组中同时出现次数最多的单词是什么。</p>
<p></p>
<p>代码如下：</p>
<p></p>
<div style="color: rgb(248, 248, 242); background-color: rgb(39, 40, 34); font-family: Consolas, &quot;Courier New&quot;, monospace; font-size: 20px; line-height: 27px; white-space: pre;">
<div><span style="color: #f92672;">&lt;?php</span></div>
<p></p>
<div>$arr <span style="color: #f92672;">=</span> <span style="color: #66d9ef;">array</span>(<span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;A&#39;</span>, <span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;B&#39;</span>, <span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;A&#39;</span>, <span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;B&#39;</span>, <span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;C&#39;</span>);</div>
<p></p>
<div>$arr2 <span style="color: #f92672;">=</span> <span style="color: #66d9ef;">array</span>(<span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;C&#39;</span>, <span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;B&#39;</span>, <span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;A&#39;</span>, <span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;D&#39;</span>, <span style="color: #e6db74;">&#39;A&#39;</span>);</div>
<p></p>
<div>$arr_count <span style="color: #f92672;">=</span> <span style="color: #66d9ef;">array_count_values</span>($arr);</div>
<p></p>
<div>$arr2_count <span style="color: #f92672;">=</span> <span style="color: #66d9ef;">array_count_values</span>($arr2);</div>
<p></p>
<div><span style="color: #66d9ef;">print_r</span>($arr_count);</div>
<div><span style="color: #66d9ef;">print_r</span>($arr2_count);</div>
<p></p>
<div>$result <span style="color: #f92672;">=</span> <span style="color: #66d9ef;">array_intersect_key</span>($arr_count, $arr2_count);</div>
<p></p>
<div><span style="color: #66d9ef;">print_r</span>($result);</div>
