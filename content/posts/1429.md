<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">帝国网站管理系统英文名EmpireCMS，简称“帝国CMS”，忘记帝国CMS网站后台登录密码时，可以通过phpmyadmin软件 修改网站数据库中的phome_enewsuser数据表进行密码重置操作：</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">1、&nbsp;&nbsp;忘记后台管理员帐号怎么办？</strong></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">用phpmyadmin查看phome_enewsuser表里的记录：查看username字段的内容.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1430" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732533366286.jpg" width="554" height="247" alt="1.jpg" title="1511732533366286.jpg" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732533366286.jpg 554w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732533366286-300x134.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 554px) 100vw, 554px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><br style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">2、&nbsp;&nbsp;忘记后台登陆密码怎么办？</strong></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">【网站使用帝国CMS5.0版本以及帝国CMS版本号小于5.0版本时】</strong>：</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">用phpmyadmin修改phome_enewsuser表，把password字段的值设为：e10adc3949ba59abbe56e057f20f883e<br style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"/>后台登录密码就是：123456<br style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"/><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1431" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732659437237.jpg" width="610" height="487" alt="2.jpg" title="1511732659437237.jpg" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732659437237.jpg 610w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732659437237-300x240.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 610px) 100vw, 610px" /></p>

<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><br style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">【网站使用帝国CMS5.1版本以及帝国CMS版本号大于5.1版本时】</strong>：<br style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"/>用phpmyadmin修改phome_enewsuser表里的记录：把password字段的值设为：“322d3fef02fc39251436cb4522d29a71”；把salt字段的值设为：“abc”.<br style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"/>后台登录密码就是：123456&nbsp;<br style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"/></p>

<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">3、&nbsp;&nbsp;忘记后台登陆认证码怎么办？</strong></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">查看e/class/config.php文件里的“$do_loginauth”变量内容。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">注意：帝国cms7.0 的config.php文件是在/e/config/config.php</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">里面的$ecms_config[&#39;esafe&#39;][&#39;loginauth&#39;]</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1434" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732840244993.jpg" width="426" height="165" alt="5.jpg" title="1511732840244993.jpg" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732840244993.jpg 426w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/1511732840244993-300x116.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 426px) 100vw, 426px" /></p>

<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">用phpmyadmin修改phome_enewsuseradd表里的记录：把“equestion”字段的内容改为0；把eanswer字段的内容改为空.</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; font-size: 14px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">修改后登陆时则不需要安全答案，可以登陆后再修改安全答案。</p>
