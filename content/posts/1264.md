<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">用sed命令可以批量替换多个文件中的字符串。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">sed -i &quot;s/原字符串/新字符串/g&quot; `grep 原字符串 -rl 所在目录`<br style="box-sizing: border-box;"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">例如：我要把mahuinan替换为huinanma，执行命令：sed -i &quot;s/mahuinan/huinanma/g&quot; &#39;grep mahuinan -rl /www&#39;</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">这是目前linux最简单的批量替换字符串命令了！<br style="box-sizing: border-box;"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">具体格式如下：</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">sed -i &quot;s/oldString/newString/g&quot; `grep oldString -rl /path`<br style="box-sizing: border-box;"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">实例代码：sed -i &quot;s/你不是人/其实我是一个警察/g&quot; `grep &quot;<span style="color: rgb(79, 79, 79); font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; text-align: justify; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">你不是人&quot;</span> -rl /usr/aa`<br style="box-sizing: border-box;"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">sed -i &quot;s/大小多少/日月水火/g&quot; `grep 大小多少 -rl ./`&nbsp;<br style="box-sizing: border-box;"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">自己扩充的解释：</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">命令： sed -i &quot;s/oldstring/newstring/g&quot; `grep &quot;oldstring&quot; -rl&nbsp; path`</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">解释一下： -i 在文件中直接替换，而不是输出到终端</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&quot;s/oldstring/newstring/g&quot;&nbsp; 替换字符串的语法，后面的g表示替换所有的</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">`grep &quot;oldstring&quot; -rl path` : 注意该命令两边的引号。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&nbsp;-r：在目录中递归查找</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">-l: 输出找到包含oldstring 的文件名</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">其实就是一个sed 命令&nbsp; ：&nbsp; sed -i &quot;s/oldstring/newstring/g&quot; &quot;包含oldstring的文件&quot;</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; padding: 0px; box-sizing: border-box; color: rgb(79, 79, 79); line-height: 26px; text-align: justify; font-family: &quot;PingFang SC&quot;, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, SimHei, Arial, SimSun; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">而其中包含oldstring的文件是同过 grep &quot;oldstring&quot; -rl&nbsp; path 这条命令找出来的</p>
