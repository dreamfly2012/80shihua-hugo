最近有在研究垃圾分类的相关问题，于是就在网上收集整理了一下垃圾分类的一些接口，方便自己也方便他人使用查询。

聚合数据api

这是一个提供各种数据接口的平台，当然了也有我们的垃圾分类接口，接口地址为[https://www.juhe.cn/docs/api/id/37](https://www.juhe.cn/docs/api/id/375)，![image.png](https://www.80shihua.com/ueditor/php/upload/image/20190723/1563851656207245.png)

1000元提供50万次的查询

2.天行数据api

这个和上面的聚合数据类似，也是需要购买的[https://www.tianapi.com/apiview/97](https://www.tianapi.com/apiview/97)这个是他的地址，![image.png](https://www.80shihua.com/ueditor/php/upload/image/20190723/1563851798825765.png)

它的价格相对上面的应该是便宜一半。

3.垃圾分类系统，地址是[https://www.zouaw.com/garbage/index.php?names=%E5%89%A9%E9%A5%AD](https://www.zouaw.com/garbage/index.php?names=%E5%89%A9%E9%A5%AD)

这个接口返回的数据是整个html页面，需要自己去解析才能获取到具体的内容，不过这个是免费的接口

4.搜狗搜索api,[https://www.sogou.com/reventondc/transform?key=%E5%9E%83%E5%9C%BE%E5%88%86%E7%B1%BB&type=2&charset=utf-8&objid=70197800&uuid=&url=http%3A%2F%2Fdevopen.sogou%2F%3FqueryString%3D%25E4%25B8%258A%25E6%25B5%25B7%25E6%2597%25A7%25E4%25B9%25A6%25E7%25B1%258D%25E5%259F%258Ep%25E8%25AF%258D%26ie%3Dutf8%26reqClassids%3D70197800%26queryFrom%3Dwap%26vrForQc%3Dfalse%26retType%3Dxml%26subReq%3D1%26dataPlatformSource%3Drubbish_card](https://www.sogou.com/reventondc/transform?key=%E5%9E%83%E5%9C%BE%E5%88%86%E7%B1%BB&type=2&charset=utf-8&objid=70197800&uuid=&url=http%3A%2F%2Fdevopen.sogou%2F%3FqueryString%3D%25E4%25B8%258A%25E6%25B5%25B7%25E6%2597%25A7%25E4%25B9%25A6%25E7%25B1%258D%25E5%259F%258Ep%25E8%25AF%258D%26ie%3Dutf8%26reqClassids%3D70197800%26queryFrom%3Dwap%26vrForQc%3Dfalse%26retType%3Dxml%26subReq%3D1%26dataPlatformSource%3Drubbish_card)
