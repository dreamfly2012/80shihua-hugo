<li>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">装了个Centos6.6桌面版,由于系统是python2.6而自己用python3.4.3,因此准备安装个python3.4,并当作默认。参考来源:http://www.centoscn.com/image-text/install/2015/1230/6590.html</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">其实原文讲得很详细了,我在尝试过程中也没有出现问题,所以就简单</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">1、下载python3.4,步骤略。我下载的是Python-3.4.3.tgz,然后解压tar -xf,命令略。</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">2、切换到root用户,顺序执行下面的。</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">make /usr/python3.4</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">./configure &#8211;prefix=/usr/python3.4</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">make</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">make install</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">3、替换原有的python</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">cd /usr/bin</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">mv python python.bak</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">ln -s /usr/python3.4/bin/python3 /usr/bin/python</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">4、更改yum的python</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">vi /usr/bin/yum</p>
<p></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(124, 124, 124);">把第一行的#!/usr/bin/python改成#!/usr/bin/python2.6即可</p>
</li>
