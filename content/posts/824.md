<div class="field-item even" property="content:encoded" style="box-sizing: inherit;">
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/css3-ui/#box-sizing0" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;"><strong style="box-sizing: inherit;">box-sizing</strong></a>是<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">CSS3</a>的box属性之一。一说到CSS的盒模型（<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/box.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Box model</a>）我想很多人都会比较烦，特别是对于新手，然而这个<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/box.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Box model</a>又是我们CSS运用中比较重要的一个属性。那么<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">CSS3</a>的<strong style="box-sizing: inherit;"><a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/css3-ui/#box-sizing0" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Box-sizing</a></strong>跟盒模型有什么关系呢？第一句话就说了，Box-sizing是<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">CSS3</a>的Box属性之一，那他当然也遵循CSS的<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/box.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Box model</a>原理，为了能更好的学习和理解这个<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/css3-ui/#box-sizing0" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Box-sizing</a>属性，我们有必要先了解一下CSS中<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/box.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Box model</a>的原理。</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">CSS中B<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/box.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">ox model</a>是分为两种，第一种是<a href="http://www.w3.org/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">W3C</a>的标准模型，另一种是IE的传统模型，他们相同之处都是对元素计算尺寸的模型，具体说就是对元素的width,height,padding,border以及元素实际尺寸的计算关系；他们不同之处呢？两者的计算方法不一至：</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><strong style="box-sizing: inherit;">1、W3C的标准Box Model:</strong></p>
<pre class="perl" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;&nbsp;/*外盒尺寸计算（元素空间尺寸）*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element空间高度&nbsp;=&nbsp;content&nbsp;height&nbsp;+&nbsp;padding&nbsp;+&nbsp;border&nbsp;+&nbsp;margin

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element&nbsp;空间宽度&nbsp;=&nbsp;content&nbsp;width&nbsp;+&nbsp;padding&nbsp;+&nbsp;border&nbsp;+&nbsp;margin

&nbsp;&nbsp;/*内盒尺寸计算（元素大小）*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element&nbsp;Height&nbsp;=&nbsp;content&nbsp;height&nbsp;+&nbsp;padding&nbsp;+&nbsp;border&nbsp;（Height为内容高度）

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element&nbsp;Width&nbsp;=&nbsp;content&nbsp;width&nbsp;+&nbsp;padding&nbsp;+&nbsp;border&nbsp;（Width为内容宽度）</pre>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><strong style="box-sizing: inherit;">2、IE)传统下Box Model（IE6以下，不含IE6版本或“QuirksMode下IE5.5+”）:</strong></p>
<pre class="perl" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;&nbsp;/*外盒尺寸计算（元素空间尺寸）*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element空间高度&nbsp;=&nbsp;content&nbsp;Height&nbsp;+&nbsp;margin&nbsp;(Height包含了元素内容宽度，边框宽度，内距宽度)

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element空间宽度&nbsp;=&nbsp;content&nbsp;Width&nbsp;+&nbsp;margin&nbsp;(Width包含了元素内容宽度、边框宽度、内距宽度)

&nbsp;&nbsp;/*内盒尺寸计算（元素大小）*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element&nbsp;Height&nbsp;=&nbsp;content&nbsp;Height(Height包含了元素内容宽度，边框宽度，内距宽度)

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element&nbsp;Width&nbsp;=&nbsp;content&nbsp;Width(Width包含了元素内容宽度、边框宽度、内距宽度)</pre>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">其实原则上来说<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/box.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Box Model</a>是分得很细的，我们这里主要分了两个比较明显的地方，就是外盒模型和内合模型，如上面计算公式所示（后面我将会详细介绍一下CSS中的<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/box.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Box Model</a>）。这样说大家可能还不太好理解，下面我们一起来看一个实际的例子，比如说现在有一个叫boxtest的Div，其具有下面一个属性</p>
<pre class="css" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;.boxtest&nbsp;{&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;border:&nbsp;20px&nbsp;solid;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;padding:&nbsp;30px;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;margin:&nbsp;30px;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;background:&nbsp;#ffc;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;width:&nbsp;300px;

&nbsp;}</pre>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">我们先来看一下<a href="http://www.w3.org/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">W3C</a>标准浏览器（Firefox,Safari,Chrome,Opera,IE6+）和传统浏览器(IE6以下版本浏览器)的Layout截图</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-825" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-model-layout.png" width="500" height="444" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-model-layout.png 500w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-model-layout-300x266.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 500px) 100vw, 500px" /></p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">上图中明显可以看出IE6以下版本浏览器的宽度包含了元素的padding,border值，换句话来说在IE6以下版本其内容真正的宽度是(width-padding-boder)。用内外盒来说的话，W3C标准浏览器的内盒宽度等于IE6以下版本浏览器的外盒宽度。这样下来我们就需要在IE6下的版本写Hack统一其内外盒的宽度，关于如何处理这样的兼容问题，我在这里不多说，感兴趣的朋友可以点击<a href="http://tantek.com/CSS/Examples/boxmodelhack.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">这里</a>。那么浏览器发展到今天，世面上用IE6以下的浏览器应该所占比例相当的少，但不排除没有人不在用。所以目前浏览器大部分元素都是基于W3C标准的Box Model上，但对于form中的有部分元素还是基于传统的Box Model上，比如说input中的submit,reset,button和select等元素，这样如果我们给其设置border和padding他也只会往内延伸。关于如何处理form中的这些元素，今日将会借此机会和大定一起探讨一下，那么现在我们还是先回到今天的正题上。</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">上面简单让大家对CSS的Box Model有了一个初步的概念（如果想了解更多的Box Model相关知识，可以点击<a href="http://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/box.html" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">W3C Box Model</a>，另外在<a href="http://www.w3help.org/zh-cn/kb/006/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">这里</a>有中文版）。下面开始我们今天的主题——CSS3的Box-sizing。</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><strong style="box-sizing: inherit;">语法：</strong></p>
<pre class="undefined" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;&nbsp;box-sizing&nbsp;：&nbsp;content-box&nbsp;||&nbsp;border-box&nbsp;||&nbsp;inherit</pre>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><strong style="box-sizing: inherit;">取值说明</strong></p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">1、content-box:此值为其默认值，其让元素维持W3C的标准Box Model，也就是说元素的宽度/高度（width/height）等于元素边框宽度（border）加上元素内边距（padding）加上元素内容宽度/高度（content width/height）即：Element Width/Height = border+padding+content width/height。</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">2、border-box:此值让元素维持IE传统的Box Model（IE6以下版本），也就是说元素的宽度/高度等于元素内容的宽度/高度。（从上面Box Model介绍可知，我们这里的content width/height包含了元素的border,padding,内容的width/height【此处的内容宽度/高度=width/height-border-padding】）。</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">为了更能形像看出box-sizing中content-box和border-box两者的区别，我们先简单来看一人示例图，如下所示：</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-826" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing.png" width="500" height="319" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing.png 500w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-300x191.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 500px) 100vw, 500px" /></p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><strong style="box-sizing: inherit;">兼容浏览器</strong></p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-827" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-browers.png" width="500" height="148" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-browers.png 500w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-browers-300x89.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 500px) 100vw, 500px" /></p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">box-sizing现代浏览器都支持，但IE家族只有IE8版本以上才支持，虽然现代浏览器支持box-sizing，但有些浏览器还是需要加上自己的前缀，Mozilla需要加上-moz-，Webkit内核需要加上-webkit-，Presto内核-o-,IE8-ms-，所以box-sizing兼容浏览器时需要加上各自的前缀：</p>
<pre class="css" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;&nbsp;/*Content&nbsp;box*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element&nbsp;{&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-moz-box-sizing:&nbsp;content-box;&nbsp;&nbsp;/*Firefox3.5+*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-webkit-box-sizing:&nbsp;content-box;&nbsp;/*Safari3.2+*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-o-box-sizing:&nbsp;content-box;&nbsp;/*Opera9.6*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-ms-box-sizing:&nbsp;content-box;&nbsp;/*IE8*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;box-sizing:&nbsp;content-box;&nbsp;/*W3C标准(IE9+，Safari5.1+,Chrome10.0+,Opera10.6+都符合box-sizing的w3c标准语法)*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;}

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;

&nbsp;&nbsp;/*Border&nbsp;box*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;Element&nbsp;{&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-moz-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;&nbsp;&nbsp;/*Firefox3.5+*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-webkit-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;&nbsp;/*Safari3.2+*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-o-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;&nbsp;/*Opera9.6*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-ms-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;&nbsp;/*IE8*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;&nbsp;/*W3C标准(IE9+，Safari5.1+,Chrome10.0+,Opera10.6+都符合box-sizing的w3c标准语法)*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;}</pre>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">上面主要介绍了box-sizing的理论知识，我们还是理论和实践结合吧，下面就一起先来看一个简单点的例子：</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><strong style="box-sizing: inherit;">HTML Code:</strong></p>
<pre class="xml" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&lt;div&nbsp;class=&quot;imgBox&quot;&nbsp;id=&quot;contentBox&quot;&gt;&lt;img&nbsp;src=&quot;/images/header.jpeg&quot;&nbsp;alt=&quot;&quot;&nbsp;/&gt;&lt;/div&gt;

<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-835" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-form-layout.png" width="467" height="736" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-form-layout.png 467w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-form-layout-190x300.png 190w" sizes="(max-width: 467px) 100vw, 467px" /></p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">DOM元素的Layout图明显告诉我们：</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">1、【type=&quot;submit&quot;】、【type=&quot;reset&quot;】、button、【type=&quot;text&quot;】、select、textarea默认情况下都带有2px的border;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">2、【type=&quot;submit&quot;】、【type=&quot;reset&quot;】、button默认情况下会有6px的左右padding；height在mac系统下会比在winxp win7系统下少1px,只有16px,（12px的字体时高度为17px）;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">3、【type=&quot;text&quot;】默认情况下会有1px的上下padding；WinXP和Win7下高度为15px，Mac系统下为14px</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">4、【type=&quot;checkbox&quot;】默认情况下会有margin：3px 3px 3px 4px,并且宽/高度默认为13px(IE6，IE7默认大约是15px，Mac系统下只有9px)</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">5、【type=&quot;radio&quot;】默认情况下会有margin: 3px 3px 0 5px的外边，并且宽/高度默认为13px（IE6,IE7默认大约是15px,Mac系统下只有9px）</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">6、textarea默认情况带有1px的上下margin；</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">最后要说的一点是，上面那些常用的form元素只有【type=&quot;text&quot;】和textarea两者是遵循W3C的标准Box Model，而其他几个都是还是遵循IE传统下的Box Model。下面我们在把CSS修改一下，我们把margin、padding和border都统一一下。</p>
<pre class="css" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;&nbsp;.submit,&nbsp;&nbsp;.reset,&nbsp;&nbsp;.button,&nbsp;&nbsp;.text,&nbsp;&nbsp;.select,&nbsp;&nbsp;.textarea,&nbsp;&nbsp;.checkbox,&nbsp;&nbsp;.radio&nbsp;{&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;margin:&nbsp;0;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;padding:&nbsp;0;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;border-width:&nbsp;1px;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;}</pre>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">同样我们来看看其各元素在firebug下的Layout分析图：</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-836" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-form-layout-2.png" width="495" height="713" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-form-layout-2.png 495w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-form-layout-2-208x300.png 208w" sizes="(max-width: 495px) 100vw, 495px" /></p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">Layout图告诉我们，其margin,border,padding现在都统一了，宽度只有【type=&quot;checkbox&quot;】和【type=&quot;radio&quot;】在不同的系统和浏览器会解析不一样，最明显的上面也说过了，这两个表单元素在IE6和IE7下的宽、高度都是15px,而在Mac系统下是9px,但在WinXP/Win7的Firefox下又是13px，这样给我们在线上的显示效果完全带来了不一样的风格，这也是大家头痛的一个地方；另外还有一点需要说明的一点是，别然在上面的Layout中分析【type=&quot;submit&quot;】、【type=&quot;reset&quot;】、button的宽度一样，但是在IE6-7之中会随着内容显示不同而宽度不同，关于这个问题大家可以参考前面我写的另一篇文章《<a href="http://www.w3cplus.com/node/30" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">input 按钮在IE下兼容问题</a>》。然而最让我们头痛的是表单元素的高度问题，前面也提到过了，在Mac系统下各种表单元素的高度都会比Win系列下少1px，这样给我们也带来很大的烦恼。我在网站搜索，看到<a href="http://www.baiduux.com/blog/author/nwind/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">nwind</a>写了一篇《<a href="http://www.baiduux.com/blog/2010/05/05/how_to_control_input_height/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">如何更好地控制input输入框的高度</a>》,作者拿了【type=&quot;text&quot;】做了很仔细的分析，另外Roger Johansson的《<a href="http://www.456bereastreet.com/archive/201104/controlling_width_with_css3_box-sizing/" target="_blank" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(41, 180, 240); text-decoration: none; background-image: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-size: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-position: initial; background-repeat: initial;">Controlling width with CSS3 box-sizing</a>》一文也做过详细的分析。从这两篇文章得知解决这样的兼容问题我们可以使用CSS3的box-sizing的border-box属性。下面我截取【type=&quot;text&quot;】的解决高度不一到致的方法，运用到表单元素上来</p>
<pre class="css" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.submit,&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.reset,&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.button,&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.text,&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.select,&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.textarea,&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.checkbox,&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.radio&nbsp;{	margin:&nbsp;0;

	padding:&nbsp;0;

	border-width:&nbsp;1px;

	height:&nbsp;17px;

	-moz-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

	-webkit-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

	-o-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

	-ms-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

	box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;}

				

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.checkbox,&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;.radio&nbsp;{	width:&nbsp;13px;

	height:&nbsp;13px;

	-moz-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

	-webkit-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

	-o-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

	-ms-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

	box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;}</pre>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">我们来看看加上了box-sizing:border-box的layout分析图：</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-837" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-layout-form.png" width="480" height="489" alt="" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-layout-form.png 480w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/box-sizing-layout-form-294x300.png 294w" sizes="(max-width: 480px) 100vw, 480px" /></p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">从Layout图明显的可以看出，现在元素的参数都统一了，但是IE6和IE7是不支持box-sizing的属性，所以为了兼容我们还需要为他们写一个hack:</p>
<pre class="css" style="box-sizing: inherit; overflow: auto; font-family: monospace, monospace; font-size: 1em; padding: 0.5em; color: rgb(131, 148, 150); margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 20px; background-color: rgb(0, 43, 54);">&nbsp;

&nbsp;&nbsp;.submit,&nbsp;&nbsp;.reset,&nbsp;&nbsp;.button,&nbsp;&nbsp;.text,&nbsp;&nbsp;.select,&nbsp;&nbsp;.textarea,&nbsp;&nbsp;.checkbox,&nbsp;&nbsp;.radio&nbsp;{&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;margin:&nbsp;0;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;padding:&nbsp;0;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;border-width:&nbsp;1px;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;height:&nbsp;17px;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-moz-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-webkit-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-o-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-ms-box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;box-sizing:&nbsp;border-box;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;/*这里需要减去border的值，如果padding的值不是0还需要减去padding的值，*/

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*height:15px;&nbsp;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*width:&nbsp;15px;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;}</pre>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="box-sizing: inherit; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 30px;">上面详细介绍了form元素如何使用box-sizing来解决浏览器兼容问题。需要提醒大家一点的是，如果你在样式中没有对border进行设置的话，那么表单中除了checkbox和radio外默认都是2px的border,这样一来你的宽度和高度在都要相应的减去4px。</p>
