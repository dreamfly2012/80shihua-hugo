<h2>前提条件</h2>
<ul>
<li>拥有一个微软账号</li>
<li>拥有一个azure云账号</li>
<li>安装了vsce工具（安装方法npm install -g vsce)</li>
</ul>
<h2>发布步骤</h2>
<ol>
<li>登录<a href="https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?LinkId=307137" title="azure">azure</a>管理后台</li>
<li>创建一个组织名称</li>
</ol>
<p><img decoding="async" src="https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/devops/media/select-new-organization.png" alt="创建组织名称" /></p>
<ol start="3">
<li>选中组织名称创建token</li>
</ol>
<p><img decoding="async" src="https://code.visualstudio.com/assets/api/working-with-extensions/publishing-extension/token1.png" alt="token" title="token" /></p>
<ol start="4">
<li>注意创建token的权限</li>
</ol>
<p><img decoding="async" src="https://code.visualstudio.com/assets/api/working-with-extensions/publishing-extension/token2.png" alt="权限" title="权限" /></p>
<ol start="5">
<li>vsce登录组织名</li>
</ol>
<pre><code>vsce login 组织名</code></pre>
<ol start="6">
<li>
<p>发布</p>
<pre><code>vsce publish</code></pre>
</li>
<li>
<p>发布新版本的时候</p>
</li>
</ol>
<pre><code>vsce publish 新的版本号(如2.0.0)</code></pre>
