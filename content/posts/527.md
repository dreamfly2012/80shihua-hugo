<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&nbsp;心软是一种不公平的善良，成全别人委屈自己，却被当成傻子。一路走来，我们用自己的善良喂了不少没良心的人，人生短暂，我们只想对身边每个人好一点，或许别人觉得自己聪明，可我们也不傻。我们可以装傻 ，但不要以为我们真傻，何必戳破。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-762" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t019a55e1763be59b97.png" width="431" height="412" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t019a55e1763be59b97.png 431w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t019a55e1763be59b97-300x287.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 431px) 100vw, 431px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&nbsp; &nbsp; 人是不能闲的，一闲就会想得太多，一闲就会感情泛滥 ，所谓矫情破事多，空虚寂寞冷，都是因为懒散堕落闲。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-763" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t01f7fa359eba974c69.jpg" width="440" height="440" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t01f7fa359eba974c69.jpg 440w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t01f7fa359eba974c69-150x150.jpg 150w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t01f7fa359eba974c69-300x300.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 440px) 100vw, 440px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&nbsp; &nbsp; 选择善良，不是软弱，因为恶行必遭报应；选择宽容，不是怯懦，宽容他人也宽待了自己；选择糊涂，不是真糊涂，因为有些东西争不来，不争也会来；选择平淡，不是不奢望繁华，而是只有受得住寂寞才能升华。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-764" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t01c6faf9d9112eff9f.jpg" width="440" height="660" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t01c6faf9d9112eff9f.jpg 440w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t01c6faf9d9112eff9f-200x300.jpg 200w" sizes="(max-width: 440px) 100vw, 440px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&nbsp; &nbsp; 当眼泪流尽的时候，留下的应该是坚强。避免失望的最好办法，就是不寄希望于任何人、任何事。没有过不去的坎，只有过不去的人。人性是极可恶的东西，它对得到的往往不珍惜。所以，当你被人伤害，首先想想，是不是自己付出的太多，把自己放低了。想要别人疼惜你，首先要自己疼自己。那些曾经让你快乐过的事，没有什么好后悔的。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-765" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t0192d8559fe54870e2.jpg" width="440" height="659" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t0192d8559fe54870e2.jpg 440w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t0192d8559fe54870e2-200x300.jpg 200w" sizes="(max-width: 440px) 100vw, 440px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&nbsp; &nbsp; 你聪明有人会说你心机重，你靠的是努力有人会说你运气好，你说自己天生乐观有人会说你虚假，有时候，你明明就是一杯白水，却被人硬生生逼成了满肚子憋屈的碳酸饮料。人一生遇见太多人，即使有些话字字诛心，也没必要活在他们的眼神里，只要内心澄明，就永远不用讨好一个不懂你的人。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-766" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t0195369100ff2019dd.jpg" width="430" height="537" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t0195369100ff2019dd.jpg 430w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t0195369100ff2019dd-240x300.jpg 240w" sizes="(max-width: 430px) 100vw, 430px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&nbsp; &nbsp; 不要过份依赖友谊，或者花很多心思去猜度身边的人对你是否真心，一个人生活不会死，体会孤单是成长必修课，谁都要经历 。人生路漫长，如果有一段实在是没人陪你热闹同行，你要对踽踽独行的自己说，走过这段就好，前方有更好的风景和更好的人等着。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-767" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t018467deeb934049e9.jpg" width="440" height="293" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t018467deeb934049e9.jpg 440w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/t018467deeb934049e9-300x200.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 440px) 100vw, 440px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 10px; margin-bottom: 10px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(102, 102, 102); font-family: Simsun; font-size: 14px; line-height: 25px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">&nbsp; &nbsp; 人生就是一个不断受骗的过程，然后我们吸取这些被骗的经验，让我们以后不再被骗，或者用这些经验去骗别人。</p>
