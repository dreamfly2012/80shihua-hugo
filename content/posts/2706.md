<p>wsl2配置zsh步骤</p>
<h2>更新系统</h2>
<pre><code class="language-shell">sudo apt update
sudo apt upgrade</code></pre>
<h2>检查zsh是否安装</h2>
<pre><code class="language-shell">cat /etc/shells</code></pre>
<p>如果没有安装的话</p>
<pre><code class="language-shell">sudo apt install zsh</code></pre>
<h2>下载oh-my-zsh</h2>
<p>国内的网络，可能需要配置下hosts,可以参考文章《<a href="https://www.80shihua.com/archives/2708" title="配置github.com的host访问">配置github.com的host访问</a>》</p>
<pre><code class="language-shell">sh -c &quot;$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ohmyzsh/ohmyzsh/master/tools/install.sh)&quot;</code></pre>
<h2>安装PowerLevel10k主题</h2>
<pre><code class="language-shell">git clone --depth=1 https://github.com/romkatv/powerlevel10k.git $ZSH_CUSTOM/themes/powerlevel10k</code></pre>
<h2>配置主题</h2>
<p>修改家目录.zshrc文件，设置主题为powerlevel10k</p>
<pre><code>ZSH_THEME=&quot;powerlevel10k/powerlevel10k&quot;</code></pre>
<p>通过下面命令设置样式</p>
<pre><code>p10k configure</code></pre>
<p><img decoding="async" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/romkatv/powerlevel10k-media/master/configuration-wizard.gif" alt="配置p10k样式" title="配置p10k样式" /></p>
<p>安装字体,放入到c:\windows\fonts</p>
<p>字体下载地址:<a href="https://github.com/romkatv/powerlevel10k-media" title="github">github</a></p>
<pre><code>MesloLGS NF Regular.ttf
MesloLGS NF Bold.ttf
MesloLGS NF Italic.ttf
MesloLGS NF Bold Italic.ttf</code></pre>
<p>设置远程连接字体</p>
<pre><code>&quot;fontFace&quot;: &quot;MesloLGS NF&quot;</code></pre>
<h2>激活插件,修改.zshrc配置文件</h2>
<pre><code>plugins = (git fasd)</code></pre>
