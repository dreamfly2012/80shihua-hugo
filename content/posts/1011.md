<h2 class="link" style="margin: 0px 0px 1.03704em; padding: 0px; font-size: 1.6875em; font-weight: normal; font-family: MuseoSlabTumblr, Helvetica, sans-serif; text-shadow: rgb(255, 255, 255) 0px 1px 0px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);"><a href="http://blog.yayared.tw/post/130326988972/ie-%E5%B5%8C%E5%85%A5-iframe-%E7%84%A1%E8%A6%96-z-index" style="cursor: pointer; color: rgb(235, 55, 75); text-decoration: none;">IE 嵌入 iframe 無視 z-index</a></h2>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; line-height: 24px; font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">IE8 在嵌入 youtube 影片的時候，嵌入的 ifame 會無視 z-index 的排序，所以當頁面有個下拉選單就在影片上方，下拉時，可能就會被這個 iframe 擋住選項，就算你對下拉選單使出黑魔法 z-index: 9999 !important，這個 iframe 還是會臉不紅氣不喘的擋住選項…</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; line-height: 24px; font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">google 了一下在 iframe 上加入 wmode 參數可解，詳情請參考<a href="http://t.umblr.com/redirect?z=http%3A%2F%2Fstackoverflow.com%2Fquestions%2F9074365%2Fyoutube-video-embedded-via-iframe-ignoring-z-index&#038;t=M2NlNzEyMjZmMGJiOTJlNTgzZGM4NzI5NGFmZTliMzg4Mjc1ZGQzNSxxUVRuZ1RLVA%3D%3D" target="_blank" style="cursor: pointer; color: rgb(235, 55, 75);">這裡</a></p>
<blockquote style="margin: 0px 0px 0.916667em; padding: 0px 0px 0px 20px; font-style: italic; border-left-width: 3px; border-left-style: solid; border-left-color: rgb(204, 204, 204); font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; font-size: 1em; line-height: 24px;">$(document).ready(function (){<br />&nbsp; &nbsp;$(‘iframe’).each(function(){<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;var url = $(this).attr(“src”);<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;$(this).attr(“src”,url+“?wmode=transparent”);<br />&nbsp; &nbsp;});<br />});</p>
</blockquote>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; line-height: 24px; font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">但是！！！</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; line-height: 24px; font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">如果你遇到嵌入的 youtube 影片不僅僅是個影片，是個播放清單的時候，用上述的方式加入 wmode 參數，會導致播放清單點擊播放時產生 “影片不存在” 的窘況。<br />所以建議將 wmode 參數放在前面，就不會影響播放清單的播放了（詳情可參考<a href="http://t.umblr.com/redirect?z=http%3A%2F%2Fcfyeric.blogspot.tw%2F2012%2F12%2Fyoutube-z-index-ie.html&#038;t=ZmYyMTRkZGVmZDZlMTg4YWFjZTYyOTkwOTQ0YmZiNmI5OGM3ODdhYSxxUVRuZ1RLVA%3D%3D" target="_blank" style="cursor: pointer; color: rgb(235, 55, 75);">這裡</a>）</p>
<blockquote style="margin: 0px 0px 0.916667em; padding: 0px 0px 0px 20px; font-style: italic; border-left-width: 3px; border-left-style: solid; border-left-color: rgb(204, 204, 204); font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; font-size: 1em; line-height: 24px;">$(function(){<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; $(“iframe”).each(function(){<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;var ifr_source = $(this).attr(&#39;src’);<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;var wmode = “wmode=opaque”;<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;if(ifr_source.indexOf(’?’) != -1) {<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;var getQString = ifr_source.split(’?’);<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;var oldString = getQString[1];<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;var newString = getQString[0];<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;$(this).attr(&#39;src’,newString+’?’+wmode+’&amp;’+oldString);<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;}<br />&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;else $(this).attr(&#39;src’,ifr_source+’?’+wmode);<br />&nbsp; &nbsp;});<br />});</p>
</blockquote>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; line-height: 24px; font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">然後我原本以為這東西只會在 IE8 遇到，因為當時測試了win7 IE11 並沒有遇到這個問題，但沒想到過幾天，有人測試發現會遇到跟 IE8 一樣的問題。抽絲剝繭之後發現，我原先測試的版本是 win7 IE11 18XXX 版本，確實不會有這個 z-index 問題，但是在 win7 IE11 17XXX 和 16XXX （推測 18XXX 以下版本可能都有這個問題吧…）卻有這個 z-index 的問題。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; line-height: 24px; font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">那就…改判斷式，保險起見讓 IE 家族都加上 wmode 參數好了…(._.a)</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1.375em; padding: 0px; line-height: 24px; font-family: Cambria, Georgia, Times, serif; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(242, 242, 242);">第一次知道會有這種隔代遺傳的 bug…IE 總是能讓我跪下</p>
