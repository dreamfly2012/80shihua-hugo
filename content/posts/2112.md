<h1 class="title-article" style="margin: 10px 0px; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none; font-size: 28px; line-height: 1.5;">首先大概会报一下两个问题：</h1>

<div id="article_content" class="article_content clearfix csdn-tracking-statistics" data-pid="blog" data-mod="popu_307" data-dsm="post">
<div id="content_views" class="htmledit_views">
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">1. no mudole named _sqlite3</p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">2.&nbsp;<a class="question-hyperlink" href="https://stackoverflow.com/questions/34295136/importerror-dynamic-module-does-not-define-module-export-function-pyinit-caff" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank" style="text-decoration-line: none; color: rgb(51, 153, 255);">mportError: dynamic module does not define module export function (PyInit__caffe)</a></p>
<div>其实这两个问题都是因为python3以上版本不太支持sqlite3，下面的方法是亲测没问题，来自（<a href="https://blog.csdn.net/sparkexpert" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" target="_blank" style="text-decoration-line: none; color: rgb(51, 153, 255);">sparkexpert</a>大神）</div>
<div>（1）安装sqlite3的包</div>
<ol class="hljs-ln list-paddingleft-2" style="list-style-type: none;">
<li>
<div class="hljs-ln-numbers">&nbsp;$ wget https://www.sqlite.org/<span class="hljs-number">2017/sqlite-autoconf<span class="hljs-number">-3170000.tar.gz &#8211;no-check-certificate</span></span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div class="hljs-ln-numbers">&nbsp;$ tar zxvf sqlite-autoconf<span class="hljs-number">-3170000.tar.gz</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div class="hljs-ln-numbers">&nbsp;$ cd sqlite-autoconf<span class="hljs-number">-3170000</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div class="hljs-ln-numbers">&nbsp;$ ./configure &#8211;prefix=/usr/local/sqlite3 &#8211;disable-static &#8211;enable-fts5 &#8211;enable-json1 CFLAGS=<span class="hljs-string" style="color: rgb(163, 21, 21);">&quot;-g -O2 -DSQLITE_ENABLE_FTS3=1 -DSQLITE_ENABLE_FTS4=1 -DSQLITE_ENABLE_RTREE=1&quot;</span></div>
</li>
</ol>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">（2）对python3进行重新编译</p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;"></p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">可能需要修改setup.py这个文件</p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;"></p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;"><img decoding="async" src="/ueditor/php/upload/image/20190529/1559120060312025.png" title="1559120060312025.png" alt="image.png"/></p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;"></p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">LD_RUN_PATH=/usr/local/sqlite3/lib ./configure -prefix=/usr/python -enable-shared CFLAGS=-fPIC</p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;"></p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">./configure -prefix=/usr/python -enable-shared CFLAGS=-fPIC&nbsp;</p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">make&nbsp;</p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">make install</p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;"></p>
<ol class="hljs-ln list-paddingleft-2" style="list-style-type: none;">
<li>
<div class="hljs-ln-numbers">&nbsp;$ cd Python<span class="hljs-number">-3.6<span class="hljs-number">.0a1</span></span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div class="hljs-ln-numbers">&nbsp;$ LD_RUN_PATH=/usr/local/sqlite3/lib ./configure LDFLAGS=<span class="hljs-string" style="color: rgb(163, 21, 21);">&quot;-L/usr/local/sqlite3/lib&quot; CPPFLAGS=<span class="hljs-string">&quot;-I /usr/local/sqlite3/include&quot;</span></span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div class="hljs-ln-numbers">&nbsp;$ LD_RUN_PATH=/usr/local/sqlite3/lib make</div>
</li>
<li>
<div class="hljs-ln-numbers">&nbsp;$ LD_RUN_PATH=/usr/local/sqlite3/lib sudo make install</div>
</li>
</ol>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">经过上述步骤后，应该就没有什么问题了，控制台输入python3进入环境</p>
<p style="margin: 10px auto; padding: 0px; list-style-type: none; list-style-image: none;">import sqlite3没报错说明ok</p>
