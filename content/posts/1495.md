<p>tasks.json如下</p>
<p></p>
<div style="color: rgb(248, 248, 242); background-color: rgb(39, 40, 34); font-family: Consolas, &quot;Courier New&quot;, monospace; font-size: 20px; line-height: 27px; white-space: pre;">
<div>{</div>
<div><span style="color: #75715e;">// See https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?LinkId=733558</span></div>

<div>{</div>
<div><span style="color: #66d9ef;font-style: italic;">&quot;taskName&quot;</span>: <span style="color: #cfcfc2;">&quot;build&quot;</span>,</div>

<div><span style="color: #75715e;">// 编译 debug 用的目标文件</span></div>
