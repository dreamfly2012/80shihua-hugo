<p style="margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 20px; padding: 0px; text-indent: 2em; line-height: 1.8; color: rgb(29, 29, 29); font-family: PingFangSC-Regular, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, Arial, &quot;\\5B8B体&quot;, &quot;Arial Narrow&quot;; text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="color: rgb(29, 29, 29); font-family: PingFangSC-Regular, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, Arial, &quot;\\5B8B体&quot;, &quot;Arial Narrow&quot;; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="color: rgb(29, 29, 29); font-family: PingFangSC-Regular, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, Arial, &quot;\\5B8B体&quot;, &quot;Arial Narrow&quot;; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">不在做事上认真，只在情绪上计较。</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 20px; white-space: normal; padding: 0px; text-indent: 2em; line-height: 1.8; color: rgb(29, 29, 29); font-family: PingFangSC-Regular, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, Arial, &quot;\\5B8B体&quot;, &quot;Arial Narrow&quot;; text-align: justify; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">本事不大，脾气就不要太大，否则你会很麻烦。</p>

<p style="margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 20px; white-space: normal; padding: 0px; text-indent: 2em; line-height: 1.8; color: rgb(29, 29, 29); font-family: PingFangSC-Regular, &quot;Microsoft YaHei&quot;, Arial, &quot;\\5B8B体&quot;, &quot;Arial Narrow&quot;; text-align: justify; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">赢一个无悔人生！</p>
