<p>首先需要卸载php7.2版本</p>
<p></p>
<div>
<div>
<p>一、删除php的相关包及配置</p>
<pre class="hljs undefined">sudo&nbsp;apt-get&nbsp;autoremove&nbsp;php7*</pre>
<p>二、删除关联</p>
<pre class="hljs bash">sudo&nbsp;find&nbsp;/etc&nbsp;-name&nbsp;&quot;*php*&quot;&nbsp;|xargs&nbsp;&nbsp;rm&nbsp;-rf</pre>
<p>三、清除dept列表</p>
<pre class="hljs bash">sudo&nbsp;apt&nbsp;purge&nbsp;`dpkg&nbsp;-l&nbsp;|&nbsp;grep&nbsp;php|&nbsp;awk&nbsp;&#39;{print&nbsp;$2}&#39;&nbsp;|tr&nbsp;&quot;\n&quot;&nbsp;&quot;&nbsp;&quot;`</pre>
<p>四、检查是否卸载干净（无返回就是卸载完成）</p>
<pre class="hljs undefined">&nbsp;dpkg&nbsp;-l&nbsp;|&nbsp;grep&nbsp;php7.0</pre>
</div>
<p></p>
<p>然后是安装php7.0版本，因为php7.0已经从ubuntu中的包管理移除了，所以我们需要重新添加授权</p>
<pre style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 1em; padding: 12px 8px; border: 0px; font-variant-numeric: inherit; font-variant-east-asian: inherit; font-stretch: inherit; line-height: inherit; font-family: Consolas, Menlo, Monaco, &quot;Lucida Console&quot;, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, &quot;DejaVu Sans Mono&quot;, &quot;Bitstream Vera Sans Mono&quot;, &quot;Courier New&quot;, monospace, sans-serif; font-size: 13px; vertical-align: baseline; box-sizing: inherit; width: auto; max-height: 600px; overflow: auto; background-color: rgb(239, 240, 241); border-radius: 3px; word-wrap: normal; color: rgb(36, 39, 41);">add-apt-repository&nbsp;ppa:ondrej/php

apt-get&nbsp;update

apt-get&nbsp;install&nbsp;php7.0</pre>
<p>通过上面的命令可以安装php7.0版本，如果提示add-apt-repository 不存在可以执行下面的命令</p>
<p><span style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, &quot;Courier New&quot;, monospace; font-size: 13px; white-space: pre; background-color: rgb(246, 246, 246);">apt-get </span><span class="hljs-keyword" style="box-sizing: inherit; color: rgb(215, 58, 73); font-family: Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, &quot;Courier New&quot;, monospace; font-size: 13px; white-space: pre;">install</span><span style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, &quot;Courier New&quot;, monospace; font-size: 13px; white-space: pre; background-color: rgb(246, 246, 246);"> software-properties-common</span></p>
<p><span style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, &quot;Courier New&quot;, monospace; font-size: 13px; white-space: pre; background-color: rgb(246, 246, 246);"><br /></span></p>
<p><span style="color: rgb(51, 51, 51); font-family: Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, &quot;Courier New&quot;, monospace; font-size: 13px; white-space: pre; background-color: rgb(246, 246, 246);">经过以上的操作，我们就将php版本从php7.2降到php7.0了</span></p>
</div>
