<p>sublime是非常sexy的代码编辑器，用来前端开发，后端开发，都可以。</p>
<p>我经常用它作为我的php编辑器，最主要的就是它启动速度真的是秒开。</p>
<p>下面简单介绍如何配置phpfmt,这是一个php格式化插件，php_bin需要填写php.exe的绝对路径。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="/ueditor/php/upload/image/20190123/1548177938875203.jpg" title="sublime格式化" alt="sublime格式化" width="724" height="404" style="width: 724px; height: 404px;" border="0" vspace="0"/></p>
<p>{</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; &quot;enable_auto_align&quot;: false,</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; &quot;indent_with_space&quot;: true,</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; &quot;php_bin&quot;:&quot;G:/phpStudy/PHPTutorial/php/php-7.2.1-nts/php.exe&quot;,</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; &quot;passes&quot;:</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; [</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &quot;OnlyOrderUseClauses&quot;,</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &quot;ReindentSwitchBlocks&quot;</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; ],</p>
<p>&nbsp; &nbsp; &quot;version&quot;: 4</p>
<p>}</p>
