<p><span id="paperTitle" style="margin: 0px; padding: 25px 0px 0px; display: block; word-break: break-all; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);"><br /></span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">1、</span>我从不担心行动的危险，我更担心不行动的危险。</p>
<p><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2、</span><a href="http://www.lz13.cn/mingrenmingyan/8609.html" target="_blank">生命</a>中你有许多机会闭紧嘴巴。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">3、</span>真正的才华体现在对未知、危险和矛盾的信息的判断之中。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">4、</span>态度决定一切。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">5、</span>世界上最刺激的事莫过于被打了一枪，子弹歪了。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">6、</span>社会主义是一种失败的哲学，无知的原则，嫉妒的教条。它的好处是能平等地分享痛苦。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">7、</span>一个通过征税求繁荣的国家就好像一个缩在桶里想把自己提起来的人。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">8、</span>大家都爱言论自由。但有些人对言论自由的理解是，我想说就说，但你说我不喜欢的话就无法容忍。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">9、</span>美德的软弱就是对邪恶的支援。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">10、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>如果给我<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">5</span>分钟，我提前一周准备；如果是<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">20</span>分钟，我提前两天；如果是<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">1</span>小时，我随时可以讲。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">11、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>无限的努力是打开潜能的关键——而不是实力与头脑。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">12、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>我本人是个乐观主义者，因为做别的貌似没什么用。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">13、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>当老鹰沉默时，鹦鹉就开始叽叽喳喳了。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">14、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>如果你感觉自己在走过地狱，走着别停。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">15、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>我们都是小虫，但我是发着光的小虫。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">16、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>求和者就是希望鳄鱼最后一个吃自己的人。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">17、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>走着走着，你会创造属于自己的小宇宙。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">18、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>民主是最不坏的制度。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">19、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>学历史，学历史。历史里藏着一切的智慧。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">20、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>伟大的代价是责任。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">21、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>世界历史可以这样总结：强国总行不义。等到他们醒悟了，他们已经不再强了。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">22、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>资本主义的邪恶在于不公平的幸福分配。社会主义的美德在于公平享受痛苦。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">23、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>不<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"><a href="http://www.lz13.cn/lizhimingyan/7966.html" target="_blank" style="color: rgb(236, 92, 164); margin: 0px; padding: 0px; text-decoration: none;">学习</a></span>历史的人注定要重复错误。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">24、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>圆滑就是能让人满怀憧憬的走向地狱。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">25、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>怀疑只能由行动来回答。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">26、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>历史对我不错，因为都是我写的。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">27、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>热爱传统从来没有削弱过一个国家。传统就是生死时刻用来救命的。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">28、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>美德与伟大不能兼得。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">29、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>简短的词是最好的，又短又古老的词最最好。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">30、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>人经常被真相困扰。但是大多数人装作没看见就走了。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">31、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>只要我人在国外，我绝不攻击本国的政府。等回去我再补回来。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">32、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>写书就像冒险。一开始它是玩具和娱乐。然后她成了你的情妇。然后是你的主人。然后变成一个暴君。最后你终于认命的时候，它死了，然后给拖到外面游街。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">33、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>战俘就是那个杀你失败然后求你别杀他的人。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">34、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>神经病就是既不肯听劝又不肯换个话题。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">35、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>世界上已经有太多谎言，不幸的是，它们一半都是真的。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">36、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>成功就是不断失败不失信心。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">37、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>历史由胜利者书写。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">38、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>有些人认为私营企业是该枪毙的老虎。有人以为私营企业是能取奶的母牛。很少有人看到它的真面目：拖着车的老马。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">39、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>我们是没说出的话的主人。我们是说出的话的奴隶。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">40、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>你克服的困难就是你争来的机会。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">41、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>在以情动人前，你自己要先被打动。在你催人泪下前，你自己要热泪盈眶。在你以理服人前，你自己要坚信不疑。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">42、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>我对自由意志和先验宿命的理解：它们是一样的。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">43、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>开玩笑是件严肃的事情。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">44、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>行政机关不经审判就剥夺公民的自由是世间最丑恶的罪行。这也是极权政府——共产和纳粹——的基础。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">45、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>勇气是能站起来侃侃而谈。勇气也是能坐下来静静倾听。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">46、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>成功不要紧，失败不致命。继续前行的勇气，才最可贵。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">47、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>如果我们让过去和现在争吵，我们将失去未来。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">48、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>悲观主义者在每个机会里看到困难。<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;"><a href="http://www.lz13.cn/lizhimingyan/3591.html" target="_blank" style="color: rgb(236, 92, 164); margin: 0px; padding: 0px; text-decoration: none;">乐观</a></span>主义者在每个困难里看到机会。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">49、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>世界上没有永恒的敌人，也没有永恒的朋友，只有永恒的利益。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">50、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>劫富无法济贫。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">51、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>政客的能力第一在于能够预测明天、下周、下个月、明年能够发生什么。政客的能力第二在于解释为什么预测失败了。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 41.25pt; padding: 0px; text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">52、<span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 7pt; font-family: &#39;Times New Roman&#39;;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>我感到自己在与命运同行。过去所有的生命不过为这一刻准备。我准备好了，我必将成功。</p>
