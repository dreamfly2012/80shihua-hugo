<div class="article-content" style="margin-top: 17px; font-family: arial; font-size: 12px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p"><span class="bjh-strong" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700;">最近几年，电影行业蓬勃发展，票房也是疯狂进化，涌现了吴京的《战狼二》、徐峥主演的《我不是药神》、王宝强的《唐人街探案》系列等等佳作。票房除了我们国内孜孜不倦的努力外，也少不了引进的国外好片的贡献！</span></span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1916" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1bb8ff6c213a062e276fa51090f93c99.jpg" width="640" height="447" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1bb8ff6c213a062e276fa51090f93c99.jpg 640w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1bb8ff6c213a062e276fa51090f93c99-300x210.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">战狼拍摄现场图</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">西方好莱坞不用多说，最近几年国内才完成票房超越，<span class="bjh-strong" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700;">但是拥有东方好莱坞的印度，实在让人惊讶！因为地理及历史原因，加上某些政治因素，我们都不是很待见印度，但是印度电影却有些不走寻常路，让我们刮目相看！</span></span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1917" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/6cdd64be2854c70f7fcf51aa78e702a3.jpg" width="640" height="307" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/6cdd64be2854c70f7fcf51aa78e702a3.jpg 640w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/6cdd64be2854c70f7fcf51aa78e702a3-300x144.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">摔跤吧爸爸主创</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p"><span class="bjh-strong" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700;">相信大家多多少少有看过印度片，例如打响知名度的《三傻大闹宝莱坞》，还有在国内超12亿票房的《摔跤吧爸爸》，另外还有《小萝莉的猴神大叔》《起跑线》《印度合伙人》等等优质电影！</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 22px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">大部分优秀的印度本土电影有一个共同点，就是揭露现实社会坏的一面，然后通过细腻的电影表演自然的描述出来，而且让人很振奋，基本不会出现烂尾的结局！</span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1919" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/2487097f9a1a9e088d9e3fd11621526c.jpg" width="600" height="245" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/2487097f9a1a9e088d9e3fd11621526c.jpg 600w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/2487097f9a1a9e088d9e3fd11621526c-300x123.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 600px) 100vw, 600px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">主人公拉克希米和美女合伙人帕里</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">2018年12月份在国内的一部电影《印度合伙人》，虽然票房不像《摔跤吧爸爸》等一样大卖，但是质量还是一如既往的优秀！虽然故事平铺直叙，但让人看的津津有味，引人入胜！</span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1920" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1e8837c1ea323a7d190c11d3c2a5f729.jpg" width="640" height="272" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1e8837c1ea323a7d190c11d3c2a5f729.jpg 640w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1e8837c1ea323a7d190c11d3c2a5f729-300x128.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-blockquote" style="display: block; position: relative; font-size: 18px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); padding-left: 30px; min-height: 24px;"><span class="bjh-p">根据印度草根企业家Muruganantham真实事迹改编。因为卫生巾关税高昂，在2012年印度仍有80%以上的女性在生理期无法使用卫生用品，初中文化程度的主人公拉克希米（阿克谢·库玛尔 饰）为了妻子（拉迪卡·艾普特 饰）的健康，寻找低成本的卫生巾的生产方法，却被全村人视为变态、疯子；最后他远走大城市德里，遇到了生命中最重要的美女合伙人帕里（索娜姆·卡普尔 饰），最终发明了低成本卫生巾生产机器，并开放专利，为印度全国对于女性经期卫生观念带来变革，2018年7月印度取消卫生巾进口关税。“权势之人、强壮之人不会让国家变强，女性强大、母亲强大、姐妹们强大后，国家才会强盛。”</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 22px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p"></span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1921" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/8c32035f9880615d98af7176ba2dcbc8.jpg" width="529" height="756" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/8c32035f9880615d98af7176ba2dcbc8.jpg 529w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/8c32035f9880615d98af7176ba2dcbc8-210x300.jpg 210w" sizes="(max-width: 529px) 100vw, 529px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">印度合伙人海报</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p"><span class="bjh-strong" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700;">《印度合伙人》其实并没有那种《中国合伙人》里面合伙干大事的剧情，电影都非常好，但本质是不一样的，本片是由爱而发，一种执着的爱，男主角一看就不是什么坏人，迷人的笑容，强健的体魄，还有一颗真诚而细腻的心，估计是个女人都会喜欢。</span><span class="bjh-br"></span></span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1922" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/e96c5713c0a535a21977cccc67503deb.jpg" width="640" height="268" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/e96c5713c0a535a21977cccc67503deb.jpg 640w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/e96c5713c0a535a21977cccc67503deb-300x126.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">拉克希米</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">但是在本片里的男主角在那个时代背景下，因为爱而努力的过程，却被众人唾弃，称之为变态、耻辱……<span class="bjh-strong" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700;">但是男主没有被这些击倒，因为他是一种印度希望，一种印度进步的精神，需要他屹立不倒。</span><span class="bjh-br"></span></span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1923" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/43e162e6824d5028f9ee105b346750e4.jpg" width="640" height="266" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/43e162e6824d5028f9ee105b346750e4.jpg 640w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/43e162e6824d5028f9ee105b346750e4-300x125.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">拉克希米用猪血实验后跳江</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">而后的他吃了常人无法想象的苦头，一般人都不会去尝试的经历，例如他用猪血进行自我实验、为了借钱厚着脸皮去帮人家按摩、为免费学到知识去教授家当保姆却被羞辱等等……这不仅没击沉他那脆弱的生活，却让他更加执着的追寻内心使命!</span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1924" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/cdec6675a57271bbc742b2d4420c1993.jpg" width="640" height="267" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/cdec6675a57271bbc742b2d4420c1993.jpg 640w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/cdec6675a57271bbc742b2d4420c1993-300x125.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">拉克希米</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p"><span class="bjh-strong" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700;">男主角在一次黑夜里落寞的晃荡在街头，他内心最后的那一点点坚持，似乎将要被粉碎，然而命运呼叫着转移，他邂逅一位很有主见的高材生，主人公拉克希米和美女帕里才是真正的印度合伙人，在帕里的帮助下一举成名!</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 22px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">但是他成名以后就会得到众人簇拥吗？没错，他成功了，并且还在印度理工与名人合影上报纸媒体！难道这就结局了？并没有，他回到在外拼搏的租住之处，在邻里间众星捧月的期待中，他展示着伟大的发明，是的，就是让他受尽委屈和侮辱的护垫，也就是卫生巾！</span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1925" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/85821f8f85c6d2a4da95257203d67f7a.jpg" width="640" height="268" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/85821f8f85c6d2a4da95257203d67f7a.jpg 640w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/85821f8f85c6d2a4da95257203d67f7a-300x126.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">拉克希米</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">在这个时候，我以为大家会欢呼雀跃，结果呢？确实是很喧哗，但都是辱骂声！这一段也让观众感受到了印度现实中的那一股执拗。就这一点，其实我们祖国又何尝没经历过，但是我们目前拍的电影基本都奔着票房，而且政治目的要端正，这样也就让我们很少能看到像此片一样的国产片！</span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1926" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/8458931d4b0bc613c81c526384af8176.jpg" width="570" height="349" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/8458931d4b0bc613c81c526384af8176.jpg 570w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/8458931d4b0bc613c81c526384af8176-300x184.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 570px) 100vw, 570px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">拉克希米与妻子</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p"><span class="bjh-strong" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700;">当然，我国也是有许多关于现实题材的优秀影片！</span></span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 22px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">其中贾樟柯导演的两部影片：《天注定》、《三峡好人》，《天注定》荣获了戛纳电影节最佳编剧奖，也是由社会真实的热点事件改编，影片很好的展示官商勾结、贫富差距、血汗工厂等等现实问题。</span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1927" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/9db5f44fa2f962dd4c347b75df082d17.jpg" width="407" height="600" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/9db5f44fa2f962dd4c347b75df082d17.jpg 407w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/9db5f44fa2f962dd4c347b75df082d17-204x300.jpg 204w" sizes="(max-width: 407px) 100vw, 407px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">天注定</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">还有关于现实题材的《盲井》，由李杨导演的这部影片题材来源于真实事件，1998年的三大矿洞诈骗杀人团伙案。描述一个残酷而真实的故事，揭露了发展中的祖国社会里冰山一角，与贾樟柯的相比，李杨狠多了。</span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1928" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/57c28300b7075887d6a37e71ca95c66b.jpg" width="600" height="763" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/57c28300b7075887d6a37e71ca95c66b.jpg 600w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/57c28300b7075887d6a37e71ca95c66b-236x300.jpg 236w" sizes="(max-width: 600px) 100vw, 600px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">盲井</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">还有许许多多优秀影片这里就不一一陈列描述，不知道小伙伴们有没有发现，印度电影同样是描绘在社会发展中进程中种种暗面，但他们的电影结局往往是乐观舒心的，是美好的！而我们是直接戳痛心灵却得不到最后的安抚，有时候结局更加让人深思！</span></p>
<div class="img-container" style="margin-top: 30px;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1929" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/c26a8fb2f1fbb484d03790d4c6d79251.jpg" width="640" height="269" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/c26a8fb2f1fbb484d03790d4c6d79251.jpg 640w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/c26a8fb2f1fbb484d03790d4c6d79251-300x126.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><span class="bjh-image-caption" style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(153, 153, 153); display: block; margin-top: 11px; text-align: center;">拉克希米被误会</span></div>
<p style="margin-top: 26px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; color: rgb(51, 51, 51); text-align: justify;"><span class="bjh-p">这一点我也许表达不清晰，直接点说，就是印度电影里关于黑暗的戏往往很少，是用一个积极的态度来揭露，就好比关于黑夜里的戏份，印度电影往往非常少，而国内的电影却占比很重，观影感很压抑，《印度合伙人》里关于黑夜的戏份有两段，一个就是男主角晚上送护垫给刚过成人礼的女孩，彻底的被认为变态，之后被赶出村子！</span></p>
