<p style="margin-top: 12px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; border: 0px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">对2维数组或者多维数组排序是常见的问题，在<a target="_blank" href="http://lib.csdn.net/base/php" class="replace_word" title="PHP知识库" style="text-decoration: none; color: rgb(223, 52, 52); font-weight: bold;">PHP</a>中我们有个专门的多维数组排序函数，下面简单介绍下：</span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 12px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; border: 0px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;"><strong>array_multisort(array1,sorting order, sorting type,array2,array3..）</strong></span><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">是对多个数组或多维数组进行排序的函数。</span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 12px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; border: 0px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"></p>

<p><br style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"/></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"></p>
<p style="margin-top: 12px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; border: 0px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">参数中的数组被当成一个表的列并以行来进行排序 &#8211; 这类似 SQL 的 ORDER BY 子句的功能。第一个数组是要排序的主要数组。数组中的行（值）比较为相同的话，就会按照下一个输入数组中相应值的大小进行排序，依此类推。</span></p>
<p style="margin-top: 12px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; border: 0px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">第一个参数是数组，随后的每一个参数可能是数组，也可能是下面的排序顺序标志（排序标志用于更改默认的排列顺序）之一：</span></p>
<ul style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; margin-top: 10px; margin-left: 35px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);" class=" list-paddingleft-2">
<li>
<p><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">SORT_ASC &#8211; 默认，按升序排列。(A-Z)</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">SORT_DESC &#8211; 按降序排列。(Z-A)</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin-top: 12px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; border: 0px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">随后可以指定排序的类型：</span></p>
<ul style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-family: &#39;microsoft yahei&#39;; font-size: 15px; line-height: 35px; white-space: normal; margin-top: 10px; margin-left: 35px; padding: 0px; border: 0px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);" class=" list-paddingleft-2">
<li>
<p><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">SORT_REGULAR &#8211; 默认。将每一项按常规顺序排列。</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">SORT_NUMERIC &#8211; 将每一项按数字顺序排列。</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span style="font-family: &#39;Microsoft YaHei&#39;; font-size: 18px;">SORT_STRING &#8211; 将每一项按字母顺序排列</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
