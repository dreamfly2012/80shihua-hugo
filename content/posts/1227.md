<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">快捷查询方式是一种多字段查询的简化写法，可以进一步简化查询条件的写法，在多个字段之间用<strong>|</strong>分割表示OR查询，用<strong>&amp;</strong>分割表示AND查询，可以实现下面的查询，例如：</p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">***** &nbsp;|表示逻辑OR &nbsp;&amp;表示逻辑AND&nbsp;</p>
<h2 style="margin: 16px 0px 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 20px; border-bottom: 1px solid rgb(238, 238, 238); color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">一、不同字段相同的查询条件</h2>
<pre class="prettyprint linenums webkit prettyprinted" style="margin-top: 8px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 6px; border: 1px solid rgb(221, 221, 221); position: relative; font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 249, 249); color: rgb(51, 51, 51); word-wrap: break-word; word-break: break-all; border-radius: 3px;">$User&nbsp;=&nbsp;M(&quot;User&quot;);&nbsp;//&nbsp;实例化User对象$map[&#39;name|title&#39;]&nbsp;=&nbsp;&#39;thinkphp&#39;;//&nbsp;把查询条件传入查询方法$User-&gt;where($map)-&gt;select();</pre>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">上面的查询其实可以等效于</p>
<pre class="prettyprint linenums webkit prettyprinted" style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 6px; border: 1px solid rgb(221, 221, 221); position: relative; font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 249, 249); color: rgb(51, 51, 51); word-wrap: break-word; word-break: break-all; border-radius: 3px;">$User&nbsp;=&nbsp;M(&quot;User&quot;);&nbsp;//&nbsp;实例化User对象$map[&#39;name&#39;]&nbsp;=&nbsp;&#39;thinkphp&#39;;$map[&#39;title&#39;]&nbsp;=&nbsp;&#39;thinkphp&#39;;$map[&#39;_logic&#39;]&nbsp;=&nbsp;&#39;OR&#39;;//&nbsp;把查询条件传入查询方法$User-&gt;where($map)-&gt;select();</pre>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">查询条件就变成&nbsp;<code style="font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 242, 244); color: rgb(199, 37, 78); padding: 2px 4px; margin: 0px 2px; white-space: nowrap; border-radius: 3px;">name= &#39;thinkphp&#39; OR title = &#39;thinkphp&#39;</code></p>
<h2 style="margin: 16px 0px 0px; padding: 0px; font-size: 20px; border-bottom: 1px solid rgb(238, 238, 238); color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">二、不同字段不同的查询条件</h2>
<pre class="prettyprint linenums webkit prettyprinted" style="margin-top: 8px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 6px; border: 1px solid rgb(221, 221, 221); position: relative; font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 249, 249); color: rgb(51, 51, 51); word-wrap: break-word; word-break: break-all; border-radius: 3px;">$User&nbsp;=&nbsp;M(&quot;User&quot;);&nbsp;//&nbsp;实例化User对象$map[&#39;status&amp;title&#39;]&nbsp;=array(&#39;1&#39;,&#39;thinkphp&#39;,&#39;_multi&#39;=&gt;true);//&nbsp;把查询条件传入查询方法$User-&gt;where($map)-&gt;select();</pre>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">上面的查询等效于：</p>
<pre class="prettyprint linenums webkit prettyprinted" style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 6px; border: 1px solid rgb(221, 221, 221); position: relative; font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 249, 249); color: rgb(51, 51, 51); word-wrap: break-word; word-break: break-all; border-radius: 3px;">$User&nbsp;=&nbsp;M(&quot;User&quot;);&nbsp;//&nbsp;实例化User对象$map[&#39;status&#39;]&nbsp;=&nbsp;1;$map[&#39;title&#39;]&nbsp;&nbsp;=&nbsp;&#39;thinkphp&#39;;//&nbsp;把查询条件传入查询方法$User-&gt;where($map)-&gt;select();&nbsp;复制代码</pre>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><code style="font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 242, 244); color: rgb(199, 37, 78); padding: 2px 4px; margin: 0px 2px; white-space: nowrap; border-radius: 3px;">&#39;_multi&#39;=&gt;true</code>必须加在数组的最后，表示当前是多条件匹配，这样查询条件就变成&nbsp;<code style="font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 242, 244); color: rgb(199, 37, 78); padding: 2px 4px; margin: 0px 2px; white-space: nowrap; border-radius: 3px;">status= 1 AND title = &#39;thinkphp&#39;</code></p>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">，查询字段支持更多的，例如：</p>
<pre class="prettyprint linenums webkit prettyprinted" style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 6px; border: 1px solid rgb(221, 221, 221); position: relative; font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 249, 249); color: rgb(51, 51, 51); word-wrap: break-word; word-break: break-all; border-radius: 3px;">$map[&#39;status&amp;score&amp;title&#39;]&nbsp;=array(&#39;1&#39;,array(&#39;gt&#39;,&#39;0&#39;),&#39;thinkphp&#39;,&#39;_multi&#39;=&gt;true);</pre>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">等效于：</p>
<pre class="prettyprint linenums webkit prettyprinted" style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 6px; border: 1px solid rgb(221, 221, 221); position: relative; font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 249, 249); color: rgb(51, 51, 51); word-wrap: break-word; word-break: break-all; border-radius: 3px;">$map[&#39;status&#39;]&nbsp;=&nbsp;1;$map[&#39;score&#39;]&nbsp;=&nbsp;array(&#39;gt&#39;,0);$map[&#39;title&#39;]&nbsp;=&nbsp;&#39;thinkphp&#39;;</pre>
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 8px 0px; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); font-family: 微软雅黑; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">查询条件就变成&nbsp;<code style="font-family: Consolas, &quot;Liberation Mono&quot;, Courier, 微软雅黑; background-color: rgb(249, 242, 244); color: rgb(199, 37, 78); padding: 2px 4px; margin: 0px 2px; white-space: nowrap; border-radius: 3px;">status= 1 AND score &gt;0 AND title = &#39;thinkphp&#39;</code></p>
