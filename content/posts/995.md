<p></p>

<p><span style="font-family:tahoma, arial, 宋体"><span style="font-size: 14px; line-height: 25.2px; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"></span></span>mysqldump -uroot -p test &lt; D:\phpworkspace\database\test.sql</p>
