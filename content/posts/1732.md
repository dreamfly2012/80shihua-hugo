<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">20190115是神奇的一天，这一天将被写入历史，而20190115.t.tt这个域名记录了这一天的战争惨况，下面让我们重温下那一天的历史。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">1月15日，张一鸣的字节跳动、罗永浩的快如科技和快播创始人王欣的云歌人工智能，都分别推出自己的社交APP：多闪、马桶MT、聊天宝。外界认为这是在叫板微信。而就在昨晚，“马桶MT”就遭到当头一棒，王欣在微博控诉“马桶MT”遭到屏蔽;刚上线不久的“多闪”，在iOS端下载时也出现了内测已满现象;“马桶MT”则出现下载了无法进入的问题。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">1月9日，微信发布了《2018微信年度数据报告》。报告显示，微信月活用户数约为10.8亿，每天有450亿次信息发送出去，每天有4.1亿次音视频呼叫成功。庞大的数据让创业者们难以望其项背。但也有人说，微信的发展已经到头了，毕竟中国的人口基数就摆在这。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">不知是不是这样的观点给了挑战者们足够的勇气，就在今天，三款社交APP相继发布，集体叫板微信。它们分别是：快播王欣的“马桶MT”;今日头条张一鸣的 “多闪”;锤子科技罗永浩的 “聊天宝”。</p>
<p class="f_center" style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: center; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1733" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/03ca20e9d0414acb8d4f5d894bb53505.jpg" width="630" height="353" alt="三款社交APP宣战微信 用户:下不了进不去用不来" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/03ca20e9d0414acb8d4f5d894bb53505.jpg 630w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/03ca20e9d0414acb8d4f5d894bb53505-300x168.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 630px) 100vw, 630px" /><br />图片来源：吴晓波频道</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong>上午10点半，王欣推出社交产品“马桶MT”</strong></p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">1月15日上午10点半，快播创始人王欣的云歌人工智能公司在深圳召开发布会，正式发布旗下社交新品“马桶MT”。公开资料显示，“马桶MT”是一款话题讨论社交App，可免用户注册，支持有趣的变声照片和阅后即焚。该软件由深圳市云歌人工智能技术有限公司开发，公司最大股东为王欣，持股比例达91.5%;其他股东包括云歌人工智能CTO吴铭、小鹏汽车董事长何小鹏等。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">据悉，“马桶MT”主打匿名社交。从官网描述来看，王欣推出的这款社交产品马桶MT是一个人脉暗网，是朋友圈的影子，所有微信上看不到的听不到的，甚至是被删除的内容都可能出现在这里，号称“匿名打探秘密神器”。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong>下午2点半，字节跳动发新社交产品：多闪</strong></p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">1月15日下午2点半，今日头条、抖音母公司字节跳动在北京宣布，升级抖音私信功能，推出一款抖音短视频社交产品“多闪”，这意味着抖音正式进军社交领域。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">目前发布的“多闪”是测试版本，25岁的多闪产品负责人徐璐冉表示，“我们希望多闪是一个无压且有温度的熟人社交产品，帮助用户缓解日益沉重的社交压力，找回日渐疏远的亲密关系。”</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">作为一款基于视频的社交产品，“多闪”对话框里面最突出的按钮是视频拍摄器，这一设计，就是希望用户在对话中更多使用视频进行交流。其中，像随拍拍摄的视频会在72小时后消失。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">表情包联想是多闪另一个引导用户用视频语言表达的体现。在多闪里，只需要在对话框里输入文字，系统就会自动联想呈现相关的表情包，无需多余动作，用户就可以选择喜欢的表情包直接发送。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">此外，“多闪”并没有设计公开的点赞、评论等功能。朋友圈的点赞、评论，将直接以私信的方式发给本人。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">据悉，目前“多闪”安卓版本已经上线，苹果版本还在测试阶段。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong>晚上7点半，锤子科技罗永浩推“聊天宝”</strong></p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">想要搅动社交媒体这一池水的，不止今日头条和王欣，还有去年就靠着社交产品“子弹短信”，在短短6天时间引来51家VC、7家科技巨头竞相投资的罗永浩。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">今天晚间，快如科技2019发布会将在北京举行，主题为“我们想跟这个世界聊聊”。外界猜测，老罗将在今晚的发布会上正式推出“聊天宝”。</p>
<p class="f_center" style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: center; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1734" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14e60cab328a41c998a1b4349251e384.jpg" width="586" height="439" alt="三款社交APP宣战微信 用户:下不了进不去用不来" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14e60cab328a41c998a1b4349251e384.jpg 586w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/14e60cab328a41c998a1b4349251e384-300x225.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 586px) 100vw, 586px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">之前有消息称快如科技打造了一款全新App，名为“聊天宝”。该软件全名为“中国移动聊天宝”，暗示它与中国移动有联系。有媒体此前曾拍到罗永浩现身中国移动南方基地，推测罗永浩团队可能与中国移动达成了合作。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">据悉，聊天宝App安装之后会覆盖子弹短信App，由此看出它可能是子弹短信的升级版，也有可能是本次发布会的主要内容。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">资料显示，子弹短信于2018年8月20日锤子科技坚果Pro 2S发布会上亮相，它是一款即时通讯App，最大亮点是高效沟通，它不仅支持语音输入、文本输入，同时还支持“语音输入、文字输出”。</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><strong>被封杀、遭屏蔽、有bug，三款APP命运多舛</strong></p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">作为王欣东山再起的一款重量级产品，“马桶MT”的火花还没燃起，就已几近被熄灭。1月14日深夜，王欣在个人认证微博上连发三条消息，控诉其公司开发的匿名社交产品“马桶MT”遭到屏蔽。根据王欣的截图，“马桶MT”的分享页面显示“已停止访问该网页”;腾讯大王卡短信也被曝封了“马桶MT”。</p>
<p class="f_center" style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: center; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1735" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/218f29a14f22492a9f60cd05e90c1505.jpg" width="482" height="790" alt="三款社交APP宣战微信 用户:下不了进不去用不来" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/218f29a14f22492a9f60cd05e90c1505.jpg 482w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/218f29a14f22492a9f60cd05e90c1505-183x300.jpg 183w" sizes="(max-width: 482px) 100vw, 482px" /><br />图片来源：微博截图</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">还有媒体报道称，马桶MT在今天试运营几个小时之后，iOS下载就被关停。对此，下午王欣团队通过腾讯《一线》表示：“产品iOS商场一直没上，并不是被下架。”</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">而作为一款主打匿名社交的社交产品，用户还质疑了目前匿名社交可能存在的合规问题。</p>
<p class="f_center" style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: center; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1736" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/f097d6629a214ae3ae9b4a6382614438.jpg" width="425" height="199" alt="三款社交APP宣战微信 用户:下不了进不去用不来" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/f097d6629a214ae3ae9b4a6382614438.jpg 425w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/f097d6629a214ae3ae9b4a6382614438-300x140.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 425px) 100vw, 425px" /></p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">王欣对澎湃新闻回应了对匿名社交的质疑：“只要是to C的产品，都要接受监管，要把这个事情放在很重要的问题。我自己对此是深有感觉的，做任何一款产品，都不能违反法律法规底线。如果(违反)的话，大家知道结果怎么样了。”</p>
<p style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; text-indent: 2em; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: justify; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">除了“马桶MT”，刚上线不久的“多闪”，在iOS端下载时也出现了内测已满、分享码无法在微信内打开的的现象。</p>
<p class="f_center" style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 0px; padding: 0px; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 18px; line-height: 32px; font-family: &quot;Microsoft Yahei&quot;; color: rgb(64, 64, 64); text-align: center; white-space: normal; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1737" src="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1d77b9b8dda941978b502c890b8bcb7c.jpg" width="630" height="677" alt="三款社交APP宣战微信 用户:下不了进不去用不来" srcset="/uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1d77b9b8dda941978b502c890b8bcb7c.jpg 630w, /uploads/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1d77b9b8dda941978b502c890b8bcb7c-279x300.jpg 279w" sizes="(max-width: 630px) 100vw, 630px" /></p>
